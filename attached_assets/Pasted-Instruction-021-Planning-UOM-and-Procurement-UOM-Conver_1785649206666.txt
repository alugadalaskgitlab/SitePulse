Instruction 021 — Planning UOM and Procurement UOM Conversion Profiles

Purpose

Restore and properly complete UOM conversion for materials such as WMM, GSB, aggregates, soil and other bulk materials where:

- BOQ/Work Demand is measured in one UOM;
- the material master or vendor supply is recorded in another UOM;
- stock, PI, order and receipt quantities must be converted safely;
- the user must not be blocked merely because the BOQ UOM differs from the vendor’s supply UOM.

Current example:

- BOQ WMM/GSB demand: compacted CUM;
- vendor may quote/supply: MT, loose CUM, CFT or Load;
- the current mapping endpoint reports UOM mismatch even though the material identity is correctly resolved.

This instruction must support explicit, auditable material-specific conversions. It must not implement the broader Execution & Supply Responsibility or subcontract-routing model.

- shared/schema.ts
- shared/boqNormalise.ts or the existing shared UOM/conversion utility
- server/storage.ts
- server/routes.ts
- client/src/pages/WorkDemand.tsx
- the existing Plant Material Master page/component used to add or edit WMM/GSB
- database migration/startup compatibility code only where additive schema changes are required

DO NOT TOUCH OR REDESIGN

- Work Programme/Gantt
- horizon bucket/date calculations
- demand phasing
- BOQ quantity calculations
- material-resolution precedence and aliases from Instructions 019B–020A
- procurement-status precedence from Instruction 020

This batch is limited to:

- planning UOM;
- procurement/stock UOMs;
- explicit conversion profiles;
- safe conversion validation;
- consistent conversion in Work Demand;
- master-screen configuration and visibility.

1. Separate material identity from UOM

A material such as WMM or GSB must be capable of being mapped even when:

- BOQ planning UOM is CUM;
- master/default stock UOM is MT;
- vendor procurement UOM may be MT, loose CUM, CFT or Load.

Do not treat a UOM difference as a material-identity failure.

The system must first resolve:

- the canonical material;

and then separately resolve:

- whether a valid conversion exists between the BOQ/planning UOM and the selected stock/procurement UOM.

Required outcome:

- exact WMM master is recognised as WMM;
- exact GSB master is recognised as GSB;
- the row shows UOM conversion required only when no valid profile exists;
- it must not revert to “No canonical material match.”

2. Introduce or formalise Planning UOM

For each canonical Plant Material, support:

- planningUom

This represents the UOM used for Work Demand and comparison with BOQ demand.

Examples:

- WMM: compacted CUM
- GSB: compacted CUM
- Stone Dust: MT or CUM, according to the configured planning basis
- Bitumen: MT
- Emulsion: Litre or MT, according to configured practice
- Diesel: Litre

If an existing equivalent canonical/default UOM already performs this role, reuse or rename it carefully rather than creating duplicate concepts.

Do not overwrite existing master values automatically.

3. Support multiple procurement/stock UOMs

A canonical material may be purchased, stocked or received in one or more supported UOMs.

Examples for WMM/GSB:

- MT
- loose CUM
- CFT
- Load

Each supported UOM must have an explicit conversion profile to the planning/canonical UOM.

Do not treat every value in `allowedUoms` as factor 1.

Important correction:

Presence of both `MT` and `CUM` in allowedUoms must never mean:

1 MT = 1 CUM.

Direct factor 1 is permitted only for canonically equivalent labels such as:

- M3 and CUM;
- L and Litre;
- T and MT, if the application has deliberately defined them as equivalent.

Different physical dimensions require an approved conversion profile.

4. Add explicit material conversion profiles

Use the smallest maintainable schema design.

Preferred structure:

material_uom_conversions

Fields should include at minimum:

- id
- materialId
- fromUom
- toUom
- conversionFactor
- conversionBasis
- conversionType
- isActive
- effectiveFrom, nullable
- effectiveTo, nullable
- notes, nullable
- createdBy
- createdAt
- updatedAt

Supported conversion types may include:

- fixed_factor
- bulk_density
- loose_to_compacted
- load_capacity
- standard_equivalence

Meaning:

converted quantity = source quantity × conversionFactor

Store and display clearly which direction the factor applies.

Example:

- fromUom: MT
- toUom: compacted CUM
- conversionFactor: 0.454545
- basis: 2.20 MT per compacted CUM

or equivalently:

- fromUom: compacted CUM
- toUom: MT
- conversionFactor: 2.20

Use one clear canonical convention throughout the code.

Add a uniqueness rule preventing duplicate active profiles for the same:

- materialId
- fromUom
- toUom
- effective period

Do not silently choose between multiple active conversion profiles.

5. Support bulk-density conversion

Where bulk density is used, the system must understand the conversion direction.

Example:

WMM compacted density = 2.20 MT/CUM

Then:

- 100 compacted CUM demand = 220 MT procurement equivalent;
- 220 MT stock/incoming = 100 compacted CUM coverage.

Do not apply bulk density to a different material.

Do not use one aggregate density universally for WMM, GSB, Stone Dust or soil.

The conversion must be attached to the exact canonical material.

6. Support loose-to-compacted conversion

For bulk materials supplied in loose volume but demanded in compacted volume, support an explicit factor.

Example:

- 1 compacted CUM requires 1.25 loose CUM.

Required display:

- Planning demand: 100 compacted CUM
- Procurement equivalent: 125 loose CUM
- Conversion basis: loose-to-compacted factor 1.25

Do not treat loose CUM and compacted CUM as equivalent merely because both are written as CUM.

The UOM/basis must distinguish them, for example:

- CUM_COMPACTED
- CUM_LOOSE

or an equivalent structured measurement-basis field.

7. Support load-based procurement only with configured capacity

For a vendor supplying by Load/Trip:

- require an approved material-specific load capacity;
- optionally allow vehicle type or payload reference;
- do not convert Load to CUM/MT without configured capacity.

Example:

- 1 Load = 16 MT;
- WMM density = 2.20 MT/CUM;
- therefore 1 Load = 7.2727 compacted CUM.

The system may chain approved conversions only when every step is explicit and unambiguous.

It must show the complete basis used.

8. Reuse existing conversion data safely

Inspect and reuse existing fields where valid:

- allowedUoms
- conversionFactor
- conversionFromUom
- conversionToUom
- bulkDensity

Migration rules:

- preserve all existing values;
- do not discard working conversions;
- where an old conversion clearly identifies from/to UOM and factor, migrate or expose it as an explicit profile;
- do not infer unsafe conversions from allowedUoms alone;
- log old configurations that are incomplete or ambiguous.

If the old single-conversion fields remain temporarily for backward compatibility, define clear precedence:

1. exact active explicit conversion profile;
2. valid legacy configured from/to conversion;
3. valid material-specific bulk density;
4. standard-equivalent UOM;
5. otherwise block.

Do not use `allowedUoms` as factor 1 for dimensionally different UOMs.

9. Update Plant Material Master UI

The Plant Material Master add/edit screen must allow an authorised user to configure:

A. Planning UOM

Example:

- compacted CUM

B. Supported procurement/stock UOMs

Example:

- MT
- loose CUM
- CFT
- Load

C. Conversion profiles

For every profile show:

- From UOM
- To UOM
- Factor
- Basis/type
- Human-readable explanation
- Active/inactive status

Examples:

- 1 compacted CUM = 2.20 MT
- 1 compacted CUM = 1.25 loose CUM
- 1 Load = 16 MT

D. Validation preview

Before saving, show examples such as:

- 100 compacted CUM = 220 MT
- 100 compacted CUM = 125 loose CUM

Do not allow saving:

- zero or negative factors;
- same from/to UOM with a non-1 factor unless an explicit measurement basis differs;
- duplicate active profiles;
- a Load conversion without capacity;
- a bulk-density conversion without density.

10. Improve the Resolve Material Mapping dialog

When the user selects WMM/GSB and the BOQ UOM differs from the planning/master UOM:

A. Valid conversion exists

Show:

- material identity;
- BOQ UOM;
- planning/canonical UOM;
- conversion basis;
- example converted quantity.

Allow mapping.

Example:

“WMM found.
BOQ demand: 1,000 compacted CUM.
Master stock UOM: MT.
Configured conversion: 1 compacted CUM = 2.20 MT.
Procurement equivalent: 2,200 MT.”

B. Multiple conversion profiles apply

Do not choose silently.

Show available active profiles and require explicit selection where the distinction is commercially meaningful, such as:

- Vendor A load capacity;
- loose CUM versus MT;
- different effective dates.

C. No valid conversion exists

Show:

“Material found, but no approved conversion exists between BOQ CUM and Master MT.”

Action:

- Configure UOM Conversion

Do not show:

- No canonical material match;
- Add material again;
- generic UOM mismatch without identifying the exact conversion needed.

11. Save the selected conversion with the mapping

The BOQ-material mapping must record enough context to remain auditable:

- materialId
- source/planning UOM
- target/canonical or stock UOM
- conversionProfileId, where used
- conversionMode
- conversionFactorUsed
- conversionBasis
- mappedBy
- mappedAt

A later change to the master conversion must not silently rewrite historical mappings or historical transaction quantities.

For future demand recalculation, define whether the current active profile or the saved mapping profile is authoritative.

Preferred rule:

- mapping uses the selected saved conversion profile;
- if the profile becomes inactive or changes, flag the mapping for review rather than silently changing it.

12. Apply conversion consistently in Work Demand

Convert every compared quantity into the material’s planning/canonical UOM before calculating coverage.

Normalize:

- horizon demand
- total programme demand
- HLC stock
- other-party stock
- confirmed purchase incoming
- confirmed internal incoming
- actionable shortfall
- future requirement

Example:

- WMM horizon demand: 500 compacted CUM
- stock: 440 MT
- configured density: 2.20 MT/CUM
- normalized stock: 200 compacted CUM
- actionable shortfall: 300 compacted CUM
- procurement equivalent: 660 MT

Never compare:

- 500 CUM demand directly with 440 MT stock.

13. Display planning demand and procurement equivalent separately

The Work Demand row should preserve the BOQ/planning view.

Example columns/details:

- Horizon Demand: 500 compacted CUM
- Available Coverage: 200 compacted CUM
- Actionable Shortfall: 300 compacted CUM
- Procurement Equivalent: 660 MT

If several procurement UOMs are supported, use:

- the mapping/project-preferred procurement UOM;
- otherwise let the user select when raising the requirement.

Do not replace BOQ demand with the vendor unit.

14. PI/IRN quantity preparation

Do not redesign PI/IRN workflows.

Where an existing Raise PI/IRN action is opened from Work Demand:

- carry the planning quantity;
- carry the selected procurement UOM;
- carry the converted procurement quantity;
- carry conversionProfileId/factor/basis for audit.

Example:

- planning shortfall: 300 compacted CUM;
- PI quantity: 660 MT;
- conversion: 2.20 MT/CUM.

The user must be able to review both before submitting.

Do not alter existing approval or lifecycle logic.

15. Vendor-specific or project-specific conversion

Do not build a full vendor-rate system in this batch.

However, design the schema so future overrides can be added safely.

For now support:

- global material conversion profile;
- optional project-specific override only if it can be implemented additively and without affecting existing PI flows.

Vendor-specific capacity/density may be deferred, but do not hardcode the model in a way that prevents it later.

16. Error handling

Return clear errors such as:

- MATERIAL_FOUND_CONVERSION_REQUIRED
- MATERIAL_CONVERSION_AMBIGUOUS
- MATERIAL_CONVERSION_INVALID
- MATERIAL_CONVERSION_NOT_CONFIGURED
- MATERIAL_CONVERSION_PROFILE_INACTIVE
- MATERIAL_UOM_UNSUPPORTED

Do not return `MATERIAL_UOM_MISMATCH` when the material is correctly identified but only the conversion profile is missing.

17. No unsafe automatic assumptions

Do not automatically assume:

- 1 MT = 1 CUM;
- 1 loose CUM = 1 compacted CUM;
- 1 Load has a universal quantity;
- one density applies to all road materials;
- all vendors use the same payload;
- allowedUoms means factor 1;
- BOQ CUM always means loose CUM.

Every non-equivalent UOM conversion must be explicit and material-specific.

18. Backward compatibility

Existing direct-compatible mappings such as:

- MT → MT
- Litre → Litre
- CUM → CUM with the same measurement basis

must continue to work without requiring a conversion profile.

Existing valid configured conversions must remain usable.

No existing mapping, stock record, PI, IRN or GRN should be deleted or rewritten.

If an old mapping is unsafe or incomplete:

- flag it for review;
- do not silently recalculate historical quantities.

19. Acceptance tests

A. WMM in MT, BOQ in compacted CUM

Configuration:

- material: WMM
- planning UOM: compacted CUM
- stock/procurement UOM: MT
- conversion: 1 compacted CUM = 2.20 MT

Expected:

- WMM maps successfully;
- 100 CUM demand displays 220 MT procurement equivalent;
- 110 MT stock covers 50 compacted CUM;
- remaining shortfall is 50 compacted CUM / 110 MT.

B. GSB in loose CUM, BOQ in compacted CUM

Configuration:

- 1 compacted CUM = 1.25 loose CUM

Expected:

- 100 compacted CUM demand = 125 loose CUM;
- quantities are not treated 1:1.

C. Missing conversion

- exact WMM material exists;
- BOQ CUM;
- master MT;
- no approved profile.

Expected:

- material identity resolves;
- status is UOM Resolution Required;
- message states the missing CUM ↔ MT conversion;
- Configure UOM Conversion action is shown;
- user is not asked to add WMM again.

D. Multiple profiles

- two active applicable profiles exist.

Expected:

- no silent selection;
- mapping requires explicit profile selection;
- selected profile is saved with the mapping.

E. Load conversion

- one load capacity is configured;
- conversion succeeds and shows its basis.

Without load capacity:

- conversion is blocked.

F. allowedUoms safety

- material has MT and CUM listed as supported UOMs;
- no factor/density exists.

Expected:

- system does not assume factor 1;
- mapping remains blocked pending conversion.

G. Existing direct mappings

- MT → MT and Litre → Litre continue working.

H. Work Demand normalization

- demand, stock, incoming and shortfall are compared in one planning UOM;
- no raw mixed-unit comparison occurs.

I. PI preparation

- planning shortfall and procurement equivalent both reach the existing PI/IRN form;
- user can see and verify both;
- approval flows remain unchanged.

J. Regression

Confirm unchanged:

- Procurement page loads normally;
- GSB/WMM aliases from 020A resolve material identity;
- procurement status labels from 020/020A;
- horizon switching;
- Other Parties formatting;
- Material Requirement creation;
- Raise PI/IRN/Split;
- purchaser actions;
- GRN flows;
- Work Programme/Gantt.
