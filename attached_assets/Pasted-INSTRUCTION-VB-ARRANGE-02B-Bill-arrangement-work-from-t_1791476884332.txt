PART A — THE ARRANGEMENT DECLARES ITS COMMERCIAL BASIS
=====================================================================
A1. Add to `earthwork_arrangements` an explicit, nullable declaration of:
    - WHAT IS PAID FOR: full service (extraction, loading, haulage and tipping
      together) OR transport only.
    - HOW IT IS MEASURED: per trip (by trip size) | per cum | per MT | per km.
    Additive migration. Every existing arrangement keeps behaving as it does today.
A2. Billing NEVER guesses. If the basis is not declared, no trip under that
    arrangement is auto-priced — it is shown for manual review with a plain reason.
    Specifically: the presence of a value in `agreedRate` or `tripRates` must NEVER
    by itself select a basis.
A3. The two rate stores keep their existing meanings and are read ONLY by the basis
    that owns them: `tripRates` (VB-ARRANGE-02A) for per-trip; `agreedRate` for per
    UOM. Do not migrate, merge or reinterpret either.
A4. On the arrangement screen, state the declared basis in plain words next to the
    rates, so the owner can see which rate will actually be used.

=====================================================================
PART B — WHAT A TRIP PRODUCES
=====================================================================
B1. `vendorBillTripCandidate` gains the trip's arrangement and its declared terms.
    Pass them in; do not look them up inside shared code.
B2. When a trip is linked to an arrangement whose basis is declared AND the vendor
    being billed is that arrangement's agency:
    - FULL SERVICE: the trip produces ONE arrangement-work row and NO transport row
      and NO material row. The agreed amount covers everything.
    - TRANSPORT ONLY: the trip produces an arrangement-work row for the haulage in
      place of the existing transport row. The material side is unchanged.
B3. PRICING IN THIS BATCH:
    - per trip: the rate whose quantity AND uom EXACTLY match the trip's recorded
      quantity and uom. 600 CFT at ₹1,250 means ₹1,250 for that one trip — never
      ₹1,250 × 600. No nearest-match, no pro-rata, no unit conversion.
    - per cum: rate × the trip's quantity converted to cum. Cft→cum is exact
      (1 cum = 35.3147 cft); convert only between volume units you can convert
      exactly, and leave anything else unpriced.
    - per MT and per km: NOT PRICED IN THIS BATCH. Such trips appear unpriced with a
      clear reason. Per-MT needs a recorded weight or a stated density, which the
      owner has not yet decided; never infer a density. Per-km continues to use the
      existing transport rate card, untouched.
B4. UNPRICED IS A VISIBLE OUTCOME, NEVER A SILENT ZERO. A trip whose basis is
    undeclared, whose size/uom matches no rate, or whose basis this batch does not
    price, appears on the bill screen with rate 0 AND a plain-language note saying
    why — in the same style as the existing `transportPricingNote`. It must be
    obvious to the owner that it needs his attention.
B5. Rows per trip-size group: group identical trip sizes so the bill reads
    "Soil hauled against Arrangement #4 — 600 CFT × 114 trips @ ₹1,250", using the
    existing grouping machinery, not a new one.
B6. Give the arrangement row its OWN `sourceType` (e.g.
    `site_material_trip_arrangement`) so its auto-source identity is distinct from
    `site_material_trip_transport` and `site_material_trip_material`. Add it to
    `vendorBillAutoSourceIdentity`'s qualified list.

=====================================================================
PART C — NEVER TWICE
=====================================================================
C1. A trip billed as arrangement work can NEVER also produce a transport row or a
    material row for the same party on any bill. This follows from B2 and B6 — prove
    it, do not assume it.
C2. A trip already on a saved bill is not offered again, by the existing
    already-billed mechanism (the `source` identity string), not a new one.
C3. The server-side gate in `assertVendorBillAutoSources` must validate arrangement
    rows exactly as it validates the others. A client must not be able to submit an
    arrangement row the server would not itself have produced.

=====================================================================
PART D — THE RATE MUST STAY TRACEABLE AFTER BILLING
=====================================================================
D1. When a bill is saved, FREEZE the pricing facts onto the bill item, following the
    `transportPricing` precedent: the arrangement id, the basis, the matched trip
    size and uom, the rate applied, and the trip id. Store it as its own jsonb column
    with a zod schema, exactly as `transportPricingSchema` is done.
D2. A saved bill NEVER recomputes from today's arrangement. If the owner revises the
    arrangement's rates tomorrow, every already-saved bill keeps showing and totalling
    what was actually agreed when it was billed.
D3. The bill screen can show the working — "₹1,250/trip × 114 trips, Arrangement #4"
    — the way `transportWorking` already does for transport.
D4. Existing bills have no such snapshot and must not be given one, inferred or
    otherwise. They continue to behave exactly as they do today.
