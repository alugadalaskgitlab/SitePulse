The current SitePulse BOM & Demand is not yet matching the Road Estimator Bill of Materials behaviour.

Please stop doing small patches and implement Road Estimator parity for BOM & Demand.

Reference behaviour:

Road Estimator Bill of Materials:

* builds material, equipment and labour demand from every work item
* if Work Programme/Gantt bars exist, uses each bar/stretch quantity
* groups resources by material/equipment/labour
* shows expandable source BOQ item lines
* shows formula: qty per unit × work qty = total demand
* supports total and monthly views
* shows individual material components, aggregate fractions, bitumen, diesel, LDO, equipment hours and labour days

Required SitePulse behaviour:

### 1. Rebuild BOM & Demand from programmed bars

For every Work Programme bar/stretch:
BOQ item
→ stretch qty
→ mapped SNL norm / project recipe / mix template
→ material demand
→ equipment demand
→ labour demand
→ month-wise distribution

If an item has no programme bar, show it under “Unprogrammed / Not Planned Yet” instead of silently excluding it.

### 2. Materials must expand fully

Do not show only generic “Aggregate”.

For DBM/BC/WMM/GSB and similar items, show the actual material components:

* aggregate fractions such as 40mm, 20mm, 10mm, 6mm, dust
* bitumen
* emulsion
* filler
* cement
* sand
* steel
* water
* diesel/HSD
* LDO/process fuel
* any other mapped material

For mix-specific items, use project mix templates/JMF material coefficients where available. Use SNL material defaults only as fallback.

### 3. Equipment demand

Do not show “Count = 1” as the main demand. That is misleading.

For now, equipment demand should be in hours:
equipment hrs/unit × programmed work qty = required equipment hours.

Show:

* equipment name
* unit = hr
* month-wise hours
* total hours
* source BOQ item breakdown
* formula line for each source item

Equipment physical count/readiness will be handled later, not now.

### 4. Labour demand

Labour demand should be in mandays/days:
labour days/unit × programmed work qty = required labour days.

Show:

* labour category/designation
* unit = day
* month-wise days
* total days
* source BOQ item breakdown
* formula line for each source item

### 5. Monthly view

Add Road Estimator-style monthly view for each section:

Material:
Item | Unit | M1 Qty | M2 Qty | M3 Qty | Total

Equipment:
Item | Unit(hr) | M1 Hours | M2 Hours | M3 Hours | Total Hours

Labour:
Item | Unit(day) | M1 Days | M2 Days | M3 Days | Total Days

Monthly distribution must use the Work Programme bar overlap logic, not just item totals.

### 6. By Item view

By Item view should show each BOQ item and all its derived:

* materials
* equipment
* labour

with quantities and formulas.

### 7. Procurement tab

Procurement shortage logic should use the expanded material demand list, not generic rolled-up “Aggregate”.

Compare current stock against each material:

* bitumen
* emulsion
* aggregate fractions
* cement
* steel
* diesel
* LDO
* etc.

Do not disturb existing manual IRN/PI flows.

### 8. Remove misleading count display

Remove or de-emphasise the current equipment “Count = 1” column unless it represents actual required machine count. For now, show hours only.

Please compare with Road Estimator BillOfMaterials.js and port/adapt the same BOM grouping and monthly distribution logic into SitePulse, excluding costs/rates/GST/profit.
