* Material demand

* item-wise
* material/equipment/labour-wise

* project-wise

Please add planning-based material shortage intelligence without disturbing existing IRN/PI workflows.

Important:

* Existing manual IRN/PI creation must continue exactly as it works today.
* Users must still be able to raise IRN/PI for any material, stores item, spare, tool, consumable, or other requirement.

Expected behaviour:

Work Programme material demand
→ compare with current stock / available stock
→ identify projected shortage
→ show suggested action:

* raise IRN
* raise PI
* plan procurement

Do not block or limit existing manual IRN/PI usage.
