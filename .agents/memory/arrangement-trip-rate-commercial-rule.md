---
name: Arrangement flat trip rates
description: Commercial meaning of arrangement-level trip-size rates, separate from vendor transport cards.
---

The owner engages a vendor to extract soil from an HLC-identified borrow area,
haul it and dump it at the work stretch. Payment is a flat all-inclusive amount
per trip, varying by trip quantity and UOM. Extraction, loading, haulage and
dumping are covered together; there is no separate transport payment for these
trips.

**Why:** The owner explicitly distinguished this agreement from kilometre-based
transport rate cards and from the arrangement's existing per-UOM agreed rate.

**How to apply:** Keep arrangement trip-size rates independent of vendor rate
cards and per-UOM rates. Storage/entry approval does not authorize billing
integration, inferred historical rates or changes to existing records. Future
billing work needs the separately requested instruction.
