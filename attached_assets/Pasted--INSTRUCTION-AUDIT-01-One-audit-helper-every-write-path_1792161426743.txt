# INSTRUCTION — AUDIT-01

**One audit helper every write path must use, void-instead-of-delete for vendor
bills, and a visible history on the bill.**

Baseline: the published commit carrying VB-PAY-03 (`b0355cd` or the commit published
from it). State it in your report.

This is the first of three batches that will bring every edit and delete in the app
under audit. This batch builds the spine and closes the money side. **Do not extend
it to other modules** — batches two and three will do that, and a change this wide
must be reviewable.

**Read before starting.** The machinery already exists and must be reused, not
rebuilt:

- `auditLogs` in `shared/schema.ts` already records module, transactionId, action,
  userId, userName, userRole, oldValues, newValues, reason, stockImpact, createdAt.
- `storage.logAudit(...)` already writes it.
- `cancellationFields` at `shared/schema.ts:15` already defines `isDeleted`,
  `deletedAt`, `deletedBy`, `isCancelled`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelledBy`,
  `cancellationReason`, and is already applied to five tables — `dprs`,
  `sitePurchases`, `materialReceipts`, `siteMaterialTrips`,
  `equipmentMaintenanceLogs`.
- `assertDeleteOrCancel` already exists and is used at six call sites.
- `client/src/components/HistoryDialog.tsx` already renders a record's history from
  `GET /api/audit-logs?module=&transactionId=`. It is already mounted on five pages.
- The DPR cancel route at `routes.ts:4031` is the pattern to follow: a reason is
  required, the old and new values are both recorded, and a notification is raised.

**Do not create a second audit table, a second cancel mechanism, or a second history
component.**

## Standing rules

- No production data changes, automatic repairs or publishing without our review.
- Migrations in this batch must be **additive only** — new nullable columns. No
  column is dropped, renamed or retyped. No data is backfilled.
- No sequence resets. Do not call or re-expose `resetAllSequences`.
- Preserve all existing users, credentials, site access, permissions and
  administrator privileges. Do not grant, revoke or reset anything.
- Frozen snapshots stay frozen and are never re-derived on read.
- **No existing figure on any bill may change.** Voiding changes which bills are
  counted, never what a bill says.
- Report against the benchmark in force at the baseline commit.

## Part A — One audit helper, used by everything

**The fault.** Of 165 update and delete routes, 20 write an audit entry. Those 20 do
it in several different shapes, each assembling the entry by hand, so a new route
simply forgets.

**Required.** A single helper in `shared/` — a name such as `recordChange` — that
takes the module, the record id, the action, the actor from the request, the before
and after values, and the reason, and writes one `auditLogs` row through
`storage.logAudit`. It must:

1. Capture the actor's role at the time, as the existing writes do
   (`owner` / `admin` / `manager` / the user's role).
2. Store the **whole record** before and after, not a field list.
3. Require a reason for `delete`, `void` and `cancel`, and reject the call without
   one.
4. Be the only way any path changed in this batch writes an audit entry.

**Constraints.**

- Do not change the shape of `auditLogs`, or any existing audit row.
- Do not rewrite the 20 existing writers in this batch. The helper is adopted by the
  paths named below; the rest follow in batches two and three.
- `HistoryDialog` renders a fixed set of action labels — create, update, delete,
  cancel, approve, reject, reopen. Add `void` to that map so it renders properly.

## Part B — Vendor bills: void is the normal action

**The fault.** `DELETE /api/vendor-bills/:id` (`routes.ts:12868`) checks the caller
is an admin, deletes the bill, and returns success. **Nothing is recorded** — not
who, not when, not what the bill contained. `PUT /api/vendor-bills/:id`
(`routes.ts:12278`), `PATCH .../payment-details` (`routes.ts:12349`) and
`PATCH .../status` (`routes.ts:12374`) are also unrecorded. Vendor bills carry no
cancellation fields at all.

**Required.**

1. **Add `cancellationFields` to the vendor bills table**, using the existing spread.
   Additive migration, all columns nullable, no backfill.
2. **A new void action.** `POST /api/vendor-bills/:id/void`:
   - requires a typed reason, rejecting the call without one;
   - requires the same permission as a delete does today —
     use `assertDeleteOrCancel` with the vendor-bills section, as the other cancel
     routes do;
   - sets `isCancelled`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelledBy`, `cancellationReason`;
   - **changes no figure on the bill**;
   - writes one audit entry through the Part A helper, action `void`, carrying the
     whole bill before and after and the reason;
   - raises a notification, as the DPR cancel route does.
3. **A voided bill drops out of the live views and stays visible in history.** It
   must not appear in the Vendor Bills list by default, must not count towards any
   payables figure, and must not appear in any vendor's liability. It must still be
   findable — a "voided" filter on the Vendor Bills list — and must still appear in
   the vendor ledger, marked **VOID**, contributing nothing to the running balance.
4. **Delete becomes the last resort, and is recorded.** `DELETE /api/vendor-bills/:id`:
   - is restricted to the **Owner** — not an administrator;
   - requires a typed reason;
   - writes the audit entry **before** the row is removed, carrying the complete bill
     and its line items in `oldValues`, so the record survives its own deletion;
   - raises a notification naming the bill number and the actor.
5. **Record the edits too.** `PUT /api/vendor-bills/:id`, `PATCH .../payment-details`
   and `PATCH .../status` each write an audit entry through the helper, before and
   after, with the reason where one was given. A status change records the old and
   new status. **Do not add a mandatory reason to these three** — they are everyday
   actions and must not become slower.

**Constraints.**

- Do not change the one-way status rule
  (`draft → verified → approved → paid`).
- Do not change any calculation, any frozen snapshot, or any payment record.
- A voided bill's own figures stay exactly as saved. Voiding is not an edit.
- Do not weaken any existing permission check to make this work. If a check stands
  in the way, stop and ask.

## Part C — The history must be visible on the bill

**The fault.** Everything recorded today is invisible unless a page happens to mount
`HistoryDialog` — only five do. Vendor bills have no history view, so even the audit
entries this batch adds would be unreadable.

**Required.**

1. Mount the existing `HistoryDialog` on the **Bill Detail** page, module
   `vendor_bills`, keyed on the bill id, labelled with the bill number. Reuse the
   component; do not write a second one.
2. Show who created the bill and when, and who last changed it, on the bill itself —
   drawn from the audit entries, not from a new column.
3. A voided bill shows its void reason and who voided it, plainly, at the top.

---

## Part D — The audit log itself is readable by anyone signed in

**The fault.** `GET /api/audit-logs` (`routes.ts:14354`) checks only that the caller
is signed in. There is no section permission check and no site scoping, so any user
can read the before-and-after values of any record in any module — including every
figure on every vendor bill, which the Vendor Bills pages themselves restrict.

**Required.**

1. The endpoint applies the **same permission and site scope as the module whose
   history is requested**. A user who cannot see a site's vendor bills cannot read
   their audit entries. A user without View Vendor Financials does not receive money
   values.
2. A request for a module the caller cannot see returns the same refusal shape the
   rest of the app uses — not an empty list, which would read as "nothing happened".
3. **Do not reduce what is recorded** to solve this. The fix is who may read it.

## What must not change

- **Every figure on every bill.** State before and after, as numbers:
  - `HLC/VB/2026/0054` — net ₹56,840.00, paid ₹50,000.00, balance ₹6,840.00.
  - Gangaram Narsimhulu — billed ₹9,41,875.40, coming up ₹3,89,888.41.
  - Nafeez — payable now ₹25,768.17. Mahipal Kalpaguri — payable now ₹11,853.69.
  - Unlinked — 23 bills, billed ₹3,11,761.90, balance ₹2,84,691.90.
- The arithmetic in `billReviewAmounts`, `summarizeBillReview`,
  `billPaymentPosition`, `getBillFinancialTotals`, `filterFinancialBills` and
  `projectVendorPayables`.
- The one-way bill status rule.
- Every existing audit row, and the shape of `auditLogs`.
- Every permission and site-scope check, except Part D, which only tightens one.
- The payables page staying read-only.
