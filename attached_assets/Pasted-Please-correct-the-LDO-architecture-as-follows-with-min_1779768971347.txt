Please correct the LDO architecture as follows with minimum UI/code disturbance.

The earlier logic mixed:

* theoretical contractor consumption,
* actual physical tank consumption,
* flow meter readings,
* heating sessions,
* dispatch norms.

These must now be separated properly.

FINAL REQUIRED STRUCTURE:

---

1. CONTRACTOR / PARTY LDO ACCOUNTING

---

For contractor/party supplied LDO:

Deduct contractor stock ONLY from theoretical dispatch norms.

Example:
44 MT × 6.25 L/MT = 275 L

This theoretical quantity should reduce contractor ledger balance.

This is contractual/commercial accounting.

Do NOT reduce contractor stock from dip actuals.

Do NOT expose actual plant efficiency/savings to contractor ledger.

---

2. HLC INTERNAL ACTUAL CONSUMPTION

---

Actual plant consumption must come ONLY from LDO Tank Tracker dip readings.

Formula:

Opening tank dip

* receipts/transfers into tank

- closing tank dip
  = actual consumption

This is INTERNAL operational tracking only.

---

3. STOCK LEDGER BEHAVIOR

---

LDO Stock Ledger should show only:

1. Opening balances
2. Receipts
3. Transfers/issues between Store and Tanks
4. One daily “Actual Consumption” posting from LDO Tracker
5. Manual adjustments

Do NOT separately post:

* dispatch consumption
* heating session consumption
* flow meter consumption
* shift log consumption
* dip rows

Only ONE consolidated daily actual consumption row should come from the LDO Tracker.

---

4. STORE → TANK TRANSFER

---

When issuing LDO from Store to Tank:

* Store stock decreases
* Tank stock increases
* Total stock does NOT change
* This is NOT consumption

---

5. DIRECT RECEIPTS TO TANK

---

If receipt is directly into Tank 1 or Tank 2:

* increase that tank balance directly.

---

6. LDO TRACKER

---

LDO Tracker should remain the source of truth for physical stocks.

Tracker should show:

* opening dip
* closing dip
* receipts into tank
* transfers into tank
* actual dip consumption
* flow meter reference
* variance
* physical tank balance

---

7. FLOW METER

---

Flow meter is reference only.

Do NOT use flow meter for stock deduction.

Rename mismatch warning to:

“Flow meter variance from dip reading — physical stock computed from dip.”

---

8. REPORTS

---

Create/maintain 3 separate reporting views:

A. Contractor Consumption Report
(theoretical dispatch norm based)

B. Actual Plant Consumption Report
(dip-based actual)

C. Variance / Efficiency Report
(theoretical vs actual savings/loss)

---

9. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE

---

Contractor ledger uses theoretical norms.

HLC internal tracker uses actual dip consumption.

These two systems must remain separate.

Do not merge them.
