Please fold these three safeguards into the same Physical Stock Reconciliation task. Do not create a separate task.

1. Variance sanity warning

In the reconciliation preview, add a non-blocking warning for unusually large adjustments.

Examples:

- physical stock is more than 5 times the current positive book balance;
- physical stock falls to less than one-fifth of the current positive balance;
- the adjustment is extremely large compared with the existing balance;
- two similar aggregate materials in the same reconciliation session move sharply in opposite directions, suggesting that the entered counts may have been interchanged.

Example warning:

“Large variance detected. Please recheck the physical count and material selection. The entered figures for 6MM Down and Dust may have been interchanged.”

Requirements:

- this is a warning, not an automatic rejection;
- do not automatically swap values;
- show the materials and figures that triggered the warning;
- require the authorised poster to acknowledge the warning before posting;
- do not use this rule where the current balance is zero or negative without suitable handling, because ratios are not meaningful there;
- keep the threshold configurable in code/constants rather than hardcoding the warning to particular material names.

2. Draft reconciliation persistence and approval handoff

A reconciliation session prepared by Stores must be saved server-side as a draft.

It must survive:

- page close;
- logout;
- browser refresh;
- reopening on another day.

An authorised Owner/Admin must be able to open the same draft later, review every row, change or reject it, and post it.

Persist:

- draft reference;
- owner;
- store/location;
- count date;
- material rows;
- entered physical quantities;
- entered UOMs;
- conversion factors used;
- reasons and notes;
- variance warnings;
- prepared by;
- prepared timestamp;
- current status.

Use clear statuses:

- Draft
- Submitted for Approval
- Posted
- Rejected/Returned

Posting must create the immutable stock-ledger adjustment entries only once.

Repeated clicks or retries must not duplicate the adjustment.

A posted session must become read-only.

3. Rounding tolerance

Introduce a small configurable stock-adjustment tolerance.

Where the absolute variance is within the approved tolerance:

- treat the material as “Verified — no adjustment”;
- do not create a stock adjustment entry merely for rounding dust such as −0.00;
- show the before and physical figures for audit;
- record that the material was verified in the reconciliation session.

Use sensible UOM-aware tolerances, for example:

- aggregates/tonnage: small decimal quantity;
- litres: small fraction or minimal practical unit;
- Nos./barrels/bags: zero tolerance unless fractional units are valid.

Do not silently zero a meaningful negative balance.

The tolerance must apply only to insignificant rounding differences, not to conceal genuine stock variance.

Add tests for:

- large opposite-direction variance warning;
- acknowledgement required before posting;
- draft prepared by Stores reopened by Admin;
- posting the same draft only once;
- tiny rounding difference producing no ledger adjustment;
- meaningful variance still producing an adjustment.
