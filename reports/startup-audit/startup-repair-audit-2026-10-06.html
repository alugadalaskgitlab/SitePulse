Automatic startup data repairs

Read-only investigation • 6 October 2026
Evidence window: 8 July–6 October 2026 • Source checkout: 50ceff1a

Confirmed: automatic startup jobs wrote production stock data within the requested window.

Production logs and surviving ledger records prove writes on 6 October. They do not establish the net stock change since before that startup or a complete 90-day execution history.

1. Confirmed production evidence

  • fixLdoStockDeductionErrors: completion log at 03:09:53 UTC (08:39:53 IST) reported balancesFixed=7, errors=0. This counts balance recomputations plus dip deductions, not seven distinct balance records. Three current LDO dip-consumption rows were created at 03:09:47.045 UTC, totalling 1,987.370000 litres.
  • backfillMissingDispatchLdoRows: logs from 03:09:57–03:10:23 UTC reported created=65, skipped=0, errors=0 and balance recomputation. Production confirms 65 LDO dispatch rows created 03:09:57.745–03:10:15.546 UTC, totalling 14,612.150760 litres quantity_out. The code counter alone was insufficient because it increments even for ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING; the independent database query confirms the surviving newly inserted rows.
  • migrateDprPlantStockDieselToLedger: the observed attempt failed at 03:11:17 UTC with InsufficientPlantStockError. The guarded transactional rebuild must not be reported as successful. Earlier runs within 90 days are unverified.
  • migrateSupersededDprs: the same startup reported 216 DPRs marked superseded. This is not a stock-table write, and does not prove 216 newly changed boolean values.

2. Important findings

  1. Conflicting LDO models remain active. The first repair deletes dispatch deductions and recreates dip deductions. The later missing-dispatch repair recreates dispatch deductions. Both types now coexist in production. The migration intended to leave only dispatch deductions is marker-gated and skipped because its marker dates to 27 May. This proves conflicting behavior, not a measured double-deduction amount.
  2. The deletion counter understates activity. The LDO repair literally adds zero to its dispatch-deletion counter after deleting matching rows. A zero cannot establish that no dispatch entries were deleted.
  3. Reconciliation is broader than the named material. reconcileStockBalancesFromLedger reads all ledger materials except legacy equipment_issue, groups by material and party, guards Diesel/HSD against negative net stock, and updates/inserts balances. Jobs invoking it are not necessarily limited to LDO, bitumen or aggregates. Current quantity sums are grouped without a separate UOM key.
  4. Startup is neither one transaction nor one awaited chain. Pre-route setup runs first. Route registration launches seedDatabase and other jobs without awaiting completion. The main background runner starts after listening, with parallel phases. HTTP availability does not mean repairs have finished.
  5. Non-stock repairs also change operational history. Examples include unlocking locked shift logs, recalculating dispatch consumption from current templates, inferring missing dispatch references by date, and remapping orphaned BOQ item 25 to hard-coded item 13.

4. Limits of the 90-day conclusion

  • A deployment-log query from 8 July to before 6 October returned no matching retained logs; the wider query returned the 6 October startup. This is not proof that earlier startups did not occur.
  • The ledger is not an append-only repair history. Jobs delete and recreate entries, so created_at may show reinsertion, not the original business posting.
  • There is no pre-startup snapshot for this event. The gross recreated deductions must not be described as the net stock change.
  • Current balances and last_updated cannot independently identify the responsible job or reconstruct old numerical values.
  • This is a current-code inventory plus available historical evidence, not a complete version-by-version deployment audit.
