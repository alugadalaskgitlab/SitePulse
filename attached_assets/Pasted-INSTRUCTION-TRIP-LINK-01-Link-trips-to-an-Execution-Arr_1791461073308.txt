WHY
The owner has roughly 700 soil trips, and more for GSB/WMM, that must carry
`earthworkArrangementId` before they can be billed against an arrangement.
Today there are only two ways to set it, and both are unusable at that volume:
- the trip entry form offers a one-click suggestion ("Link this arrangement to the
  receipt"), which only helps at entry time;
- the Materials Received edit form sets it one trip at a time.

The Set-roles dialog on Material Trips cannot set it at all: `tripRolePayload`
(`shared/tripTransportRoles.ts` ~line 56) writes only the role fields, and the
dialog states "Quantities and work context are not changed."

So the owner is editing trips one by one on the wrong screen. This batch gives him
the same bulk tool he already has for material source.
