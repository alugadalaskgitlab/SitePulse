INSTRUCTION DPR-PAGE-01-FIX: Summary tiles must list ALL activities and ALL materials (small follow-up to DPR-PAGE-01)

READ THIS FIRST
Small display-only change to the DPR Site Report summary tiles built in DPR-PAGE-01 (client/src/pages/SiteReport.tsx, the dpr-management-summary block, and client/src/lib/dprManagementPresentation.ts if a helper is cleaner). Nothing else changes. No stored value, calculation, schema, readiness or other page is touched. Do not publish automatically. STOP after this and report back.

WHY
Today the "Work done" tile shows one quantity and names the activity only when there is exactly ONE; with several it collapses to a count. The "Bulk received" tile does the same ("2 materials"). Management looks at this strip to see, at a glance, WHICH activities were done with WHAT quantity, and WHICH materials came in with how much and how many trips. A bare count does not answer that. Quantities of different items must never be added together (Cum, Sqm, Ha, MT are not addable). No money/value is to be shown anywhere on this page — rates and value belong to the reports, not the DPR.

A. WORK DONE TILE
- One activity (unchanged): big "56.25 Cum", small "Work done · Wet Mix Macadam".
- Two or more: big "3 items", then a small list on its own line(s) inside the same tile:
  "WMM 56.25 Cum · GSB 120 Cum · Clearing 0.5 Ha"
  Use the short item name (shortItemName) and the same quantity text the Work done table shows (managementQuantity). Show up to 3 items, then "+2 more". Order: largest contribution first is NOT required — keep the order they appear in the DPR.
- Activities flagged No site work are excluded from the list and from the count; if every activity is No site work, the tile shows "No site work".
- Incidental activities are included but marked, e.g. "Diversion 40 Sqm (incidental)".

B. BULK RECEIVED TILE
- One material (unchanged): big "141.03 MT", small "Bulk received · WMM".
- Two or more: big "2 materials", then a small list:
  "WMM 141.03 MT (5 trips) · Soil 60 MT (3 trips)"
  Same grouping already used by the Materials table (summarizeReceived). Up to 3, then "+n more". Include the trip count for each material, since trips are what management checks against the transporter.
- Add the trip count to the one-material case too: small line "Bulk received · WMM · 5 trips".

C. WHATSAPP SHARE TEXT
Make the shared text match: list every activity with its quantity (not just the first), and every material with quantity and trips. No truncation in the text — the "+n more" rule applies only on screen.

D. LAYOUT
The tiles must stay one row on desktop and wrap cleanly on a phone. If a list makes a tile too tall, let that tile grow and keep the others aligned to the top; do not shrink the big number. Small list text: 12–12.5px, dark slate, not light grey.

WHAT NOT TO DO

- Do not total quantities across different items or units.
- Do not change the Work done or Materials tables themselves, or any other section.
