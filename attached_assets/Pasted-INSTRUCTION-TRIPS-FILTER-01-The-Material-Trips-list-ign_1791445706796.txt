=====================================================================
THE DEFECT
=====================================================================
The Material Trips screen sends five filters. The list endpoint reads two of them and silently drops the rest.

`client/src/pages/SiteMaterialTrips.tsx` `buildTripsUrl()` (~line 244) correctly sends:
  dateFrom, dateTo, site, material, **vehicleNumber**, **supplier**, **onlyUnassigned**

`server/routes.ts` `app.get("/api/site-material-trips")` (~line 814) builds its `filters` object with site, material, dateFrom, dateTo and the linkage ids only. **`vehicleNumber`, `supplier` and `onlyUnassigned` are never read.**

`server/storage.ts` `getSiteMaterialTrips` (~line 12500) does not accept or apply them either — not in the interface declaration (~line 1379), not in the implementation.

Observed: with Transporter = "FASIUDDIN" typed in, the list still returns 545 trips and shows rows transported by GANGARAM NARSIMHULU and G SANGANNA. Same for Vehicle number. Same for the "Only trips without a material source" checkbox.

WHY THIS IS URGENT AND NOT COSMETIC
=====================================================================
The bulk material-source tool on this same screen **does** honour all three. `app.post("/api/site-material-trips/material-source/bulk")` accepts `vehicleNumber`, `supplier` and `onlyUnassigned` in its schema and `bulkAssignSiteMaterialTripMaterialSource` applies them.

- The rows the owner is looking at are filtered by date/site/material ONLY.
- The confirm dialog says "Assign to {filteredTrips.length} matching trips" using that same unfiltered count.
- The bulk write then applies the transporter and vehicle filters the list ignored, and changes a different set of rows.

The owner is about to point this tool at roughly 700 soil trips. He must not be shown one set of rows and have another set written. Fix the list so both sides mean the same thing.
