# INSTRUCTION — VB-HIRE-07

**Propose a deduction for operator-unavailable days, close the breakdown gap, and
give the recorded statuses a report of their own. Nothing is deducted
automatically, ever.**

Baseline: the commit published for VB-HIRE-06. State that commit in your report.

**Read the whole instruction before changing anything.** Our earlier draft of this
work assumed the breakdown deduction had to be built. It does not. It already
exists and works. Building a second one would double-count. The correctness of
this batch depends on you reusing the existing exception pipeline rather than
writing a parallel one.

## What already exists — do not rebuild any of it

In `shared/hireBilling.ts`, `calculateHireBilling` already runs a complete
exception pipeline:

- Breakdown exceptions are raised from maintenance rows:
  `exceptions.push({ sourceType: "maintenance", exceptionType: "breakdown", … })`
- A per-day rate is already derived from the agreed terms:
  `terms.billingBasis === "monthly" ? monthlyDailyRate(item.date, terms) : terms.rate`
- An hour-based suggestion already exists, gated on `terms.breakdownHoursPerDay`
  being between 10 and 12.
- Decisions already resolve as `full_day | half_day | hours | manual | none`, and
  `deductionAmount` accumulates from them.
- `terms.breakdownDeductionEnabled` already blocks a breakdown deduction entirely.
- A monthly grace allowance and a duplicate-source guard
  (`DUPLICATE BREAKDOWN SOURCE — DAY ALREADY RESOLVED`) already exist.
- The total is already capped: `money(Math.min(grossAmount, deductionAmount))`.
- The presence of exceptions already drives the review workflow:
  `requiresReview: exceptions.length > 0`.

`hire_statement_exceptions` already persists all of it: `sourceType`
(`usage | maintenance | manual`), `exceptionType`, `exceptionDate`, `decision`,
`manualDeductionAmount`, `remarks`, `resolvedBy`, `resolvedAt`.

**So this batch adds two exception sources to that pipeline. It adds no new
arithmetic, no new rate, no new table and no new deduction mechanism.**

## Part A — Operator / Driver Unavailable days

**What is missing.** VB-HIRE-06 made `idle_no_operator` visible on the bill and
counted in its own column. Nothing raises an exception for it, so there is
nothing to decide and nothing to deduct. The user has been typing these in by
hand as a bill-level adjustment — for example
*"OPERATOR UNAVAILABLE FOR TWO DAYS (13TH&14TH): −10,000.00"* on
`HLC/VB/2026/0058`.

**Required.**

1. Where a day in the statement period carries a recorded DPR status of
   `idle_no_operator`, raise one exception into the existing pipeline with
   `sourceType: "usage"`, `sourceId` set to the usage/log row, a new
   `exceptionType: "operator_unavailable"`, the date, and a plain description.
2. Its suggested amount uses the **existing** day rate — `monthlyDailyRate(date, terms)`
   on a monthly basis, `terms.rate` otherwise. **Do not introduce a new rate, a new
   divisor, or a per-day price not derived from the agreed terms.**
3. **The default decision is `none`.** Nothing is deducted until a person chooses
   `full_day`, `half_day` or `manual`. There is no automatic grace and no
   automatic deduction for this type — unlike breakdown, which has both.
4. **Do not add a schema column to enable or disable this.** No migration. The
   `none` default is the control.
5. One exception per day per statement. If a day already carries an exception of
   this type, do not raise a second.

## Part B — A DPR breakdown with no maintenance log raises nothing

**The gap.** Breakdown exceptions come from maintenance rows only. A day the site
marked `breakdown` in the DPR dropdown, where nobody also wrote a maintenance
log, produces no exception, no suggestion and no review flag. VB-HIRE-06 made
that day visible in the Breakdown Days column — but it is counted and never
decided.

**Required.**

1. Where a day carries a recorded DPR status of `breakdown` and **no**
   maintenance-sourced breakdown exception exists for that same date, raise a
   `breakdown` exception with `sourceType: "usage"`.
2. It must then flow through the **existing** breakdown handling unchanged —
   the same day rate, the same `breakdownDeductionEnabled` gate, the same grace
   allowance, the same decisions.
3. **Use the existing duplicate guard.** Where both a maintenance log and a DPR
   breakdown status exist for one date, exactly one exception is decided and the
   other is marked duplicate, as the code already does. **Double-counting a
   breakdown day is the specific failure to guard against in this part.**
4. No `downtimeHours` is invented. A DPR-sourced breakdown with no recorded
   downtime simply has none, and the hour-based suggestion does not apply to it.

---

## Part C — Idle · No Work Available stays free of money

**This is not negotiable and it is not a preference.**

Where a day is recorded `idle_no_work`, **raise no exception and propose no
amount.** The machine was present and available and HLC had no work for it. That
is HLC's own cost. It is already counted and displayed in its own column by
VB-HIRE-06, and that is the whole of what the application should do with it.

Proposing a deduction against a day the vendor made his machine available would
be proposing that HLC short its vendors, and it is an argument HLC would lose.

A day with **no status recorded** likewise raises nothing. A blank day is not
evidence.

## Part D — Show the workings, and warn about the manual lines

1. **Show the workings** beside each suggestion: the date, the day rate, which
   divisor produced it, and the resulting amount. The user must be able to check
   the arithmetic without opening anything else.
2. **Refuse to suggest where the basis is unavailable** — no rate, no divisor, or
   a period whose terms cannot be established. Show the days and state that the
   amount cannot be established. **Never substitute a default.** Follow the
   existing diesel wording and shape, including its refusals:
   - `"A suggested diesel recovery cannot be accepted because the applicable HSD rate is unavailable."`
   - `"An explicit non-negative diesel recovery amount is required."`
   Use the equivalent sentences for this type. Do not invent a different style of
   refusal.
3. **Warn about possible double-counting with hand-typed adjustments.** Bills
   carry free-text `additionalAdjustments`, and the user has been entering
   operator-unavailable deductions there. You cannot reliably match free text to
   a date. **Do not try, and do not suppress either line.** Instead: where a
   statement has one or more operator-unavailable suggestions and its bill also
   carries any additional adjustment, show a plain warning naming the dates and
   the adjustment, and leave both for the user to resolve.

## Part E — An Equipment Status report

**This is why it belongs in this batch.** From the moment Part A is live, a day
the site ticked can take money off a vendor. Today there is **no report anywhere**
that lists those days. The status appears only on the DPR itself, as a machine
count on the Site Report, and — after VB-HIRE-06 — on the bill. So when a vendor
asks *"which two days was my operator absent?"*, the only evidence is the bill plus
opening each DPR one at a time. A deduction you cannot produce evidence for is a
deduction you will lose.

**Almost all of this already exists. Build only the screen.**

- `GET /api/reports/equipment-status?dateFrom=&dateTo=` already exists in
  `server/routes.ts`. It validates both dates, applies
  `getPermittedSiteNames(req)`, and returns `FleetStatusResponse`.
- `storage.getFleetEquipmentStatus` already applies the full site-authorisation
  boundary, including the rule that a DPR-linked usage row is authorised by its
  DPR's site and that draft, deleted, cancelled and superseded DPRs are excluded.
- `buildFleetEquipmentStatus` already returns, per machine: `name`, `ownership`,
  `vendorName`, `meterType`, a `summary` of `working / idleNoWork /
  idleNoOperator / breakdown / loggedUnspecified / notLogged`, and a `days` array
  carrying each date's `status`, `reason`, `legacyLogged` and `conflict`.

**No client page calls this endpoint.** That is the entire gap.

**Required.**

1. Add an **Equipment Status** report page and list it in `ReportsHub.tsx`
   alongside Equipment Performance, following the same `returnTo` pattern as the
   existing entries.
2. Filters: a date range, and an optional single machine. Default to the current
   month.
3. Show a **summary row per machine** with all six counts as separate columns —
   Working, Idle · No Work Available, Idle · Operator Unavailable, Breakdown,
   Logged (status not specified), Not Logged. Use the same wording as the bill so
   the two agree.
4. Expanding a machine shows its **day-by-day list**: date, status, and the
   recorded reason where one exists. Mark a day flagged `conflict` plainly, and
   do not hide it.
5. **Print and Excel export**, so a day list can be put in front of a vendor.
6. **Read-only. This report writes nothing.** It creates no exception, changes no
   bill, and does not alter any status.

**Constraints.**

- **Do not write a new query or a second authorisation path.** Call the existing
  endpoint. Its site-scope rules are deliberate and must not be widened,
  bypassed or reimplemented on the client.
- **Not Logged means not logged.** Never render it as Idle, Breakdown or Working,
  and never infer a status from activity rows, maintenance records or anything
  else. That count is the application stating what it does not know, and it is the
  most useful figure on the page.
- Respect the existing permission gate on reports. A user who cannot see a site's
  data must not see its machines here.
- No money, rate or ₹ figure anywhere on this report. It reports facts, not
  amounts.

## What must not change

- **Diesel recovery arithmetic.** Do not touch `authoritativeHireDieselPeriod`,
  `calculateHireDieselPricing` or `applyAuthoritativeDieselPeriod`.
- **Existing breakdown arithmetic.** The day rate, the hour-based suggestion, the
  grace allowance, the `breakdownDeductionEnabled` gate and the duplicate guard
  all stay exactly as they are. You are adding a source, not changing a rule.
- **The deduction cap** `money(Math.min(grossAmount, deductionAmount))`.
- **Every figure on every saved bill.** `HLC/VB/2026/0058` — bill total
  **₹1,20,000**, net payable **₹1,07,600**. TRACTOR-RATNAM September — gross
  **₹30,000**, final recovery **₹0.00**. State all of these before and after.
- The read-only property: projecting a review must not alter the saved bill.
- No permission check and no site-scope check.

**One behaviour change you must state explicitly in your report.** Raising
exceptions sets `requiresReview: true`, which moves a statement from
`draft → approved → billed` to `draft → reviewed → approved → billed`. So bills
with operator-unavailable or DPR-breakdown days will now carry a review step they
did not carry before. Confirm this affects **new** calculations only and that no
saved statement's workflow changed.
