# VB-HIRE-TERMS-01 — Hire Basis and Rate per Vendor Bill

**Objective:** Allow the same hired equipment to be billed on different hire bases and rates for different assignments or billing periods, without changing Equipment Master or historical records.

This is a focused correction to the existing Equipment Hire Vendor Bill workflow. Do not create a new billing module.

## 1. Equipment Master — Keep Default Terms Only

Retain the existing Hire Basis, Hire Rate and Hire Dates in Equipment Master for compatibility.

However, treat these as **default suggestions**, not compulsory terms for every Vendor Bill.

Do not modify existing Equipment Master records or automatically convert any equipment from Trip to Daily Hire.

Equipment identity, registration number and verified vendor/owner association must remain independent of its billing basis.

## 2. Vendor Bill — Select Actual Hire Terms

In **New Vendor Bill → Equipment Hire**, after selecting the vendor and equipment, display:

- **Hire Basis:** Trip / Daily / Hourly / Monthly
- **Hire Rate (₹):** Editable amount
- **Rate Unit:** Automatically displays ₹/Trip, ₹/Day, ₹/Hour or ₹/Month
- **Billing Period:** From and To dates

Pre-fill Hire Basis and Rate from Equipment Master when available, but allow the authorised bill preparer to change them for this bill.

The selected Hire Basis and Rate must govern the calculation—not the current Equipment Master defaults.

Where multiple equipment items are included, each must have its own terms.

## 3. Calculation and Evidence

Reuse existing SitePulse calculations and operational records.

- **Trip Basis:** Use eligible recorded trips and the agreed rate per trip.
- **Daily Basis:** Use the existing VB-TIPPER-03 date-wise review, confirmed trip evidence and Full/Half/Excluded decisions. Multiple trips by the same tipper on one date must not multiply the daily charge.
- **Hourly Basis:** Use actual recorded working hours. Never infer hours from the number of trips.
- **Monthly Basis:** Use the existing monthly hire calculation, divisor rules, daily evidence, breakdown and idle-day treatment.

Show the calculation clearly before adding the charge to the bill:

**Hire Basis | Rate | Billable Quantity | Unit | Gross Amount**

Example:

Daily | ₹5,000 | 8.5 | Days | ₹42,500

Do not fabricate missing trips, hours or daily activity.

## 4. Preserve Terms with Each Bill

When saving the bill, preserve the selected Hire Basis, Rate, Billing Period, Billable Quantity and calculation evidence in its existing hire-statement/bill snapshot structure.

When reopening a Draft, Verified, Approved or Paid bill, display the original saved terms—not newly changed Equipment Master defaults.

Existing saved bills must retain their current calculation and financial figures.

An authorised user editing an eligible Draft may change its hire terms, with the affected calculation reviewed again before saving.

## 5. Vendor and Trip Linking

Keep VB-TIPPER-03's existing confirmed Hire Billing Links and reviewed vendor identity associations.

Selecting Daily Hire at the bill stage must make the correctly linked tipper eligible even if its Equipment Master default says Trip Hire.

Likewise, switching between billing bases must not require:

- Creating another equipment record
- Renaming the vendor
- Re-linking already confirmed trips
- Editing submitted DPRs
- Changing historical Equipment Master terms

Keep duplicate-billing protection across billing bases. A switch from Trip to Daily must not allow the same payable equipment activity to be charged twice. Flag overlapping existing charges for explicit review rather than silently permitting them.

## 6. User Interface — Keep It Simple

Use the existing Equipment Hire billing form.

Do not expose internal terms such as groups, snapshots, overrides or fractions unless required in an advanced review.

A bill preparer should follow this straightforward sequence:

**Vendor → Equipment → Hire Basis → Rate → Period → Activity Review → Bill Calculation → Save Draft**

The selected basis and rate should also be visible in the saved Bill Detail and vendor-facing PDF/Excel.

## 7. Verification

Test these specific situations:

1. Equipment Master default Trip; current bill Daily.
2. Equipment Master default Monthly; current bill Hourly.
3. One tipper billed on different bases in different non-overlapping periods.
4. Multiple tippers belonging to one vendor with different hire bases and rates.
5. Daily Hire correctly reading confirmed historical Material Trips.
6. Existing billed activity preventing duplicate charging across hire bases.
7. Saving and reopening a Draft preserves the selected terms.
8. Editing a Draft recalculates only that draft.
9. Existing Verified, Approved and Paid bills remain unchanged.
10. Current monthly hire, recovery and saved-calendar workflows continue functioning.

Use focused tests and one production build.

Do not run the full suite, perform historical backfills or alter unrelated modules.

## 8. Do Not Touch

- Existing historical Vendor Bills or payment statuses
- Equipment Master saved defaults
- Submitted DPRs
- Material Trips and receipts
- Vendor Master identities or confirmed links
- Diesel, procurement, inventory or Tally integration
- Unpaid/All overview, tax summary or financial exports
- Existing approval and financial permissions

Do not publish automatically or perform destructive database migrations.

Report any necessary schema/index changes before publication.

**STOP after implementation, focused verification and one build.**
