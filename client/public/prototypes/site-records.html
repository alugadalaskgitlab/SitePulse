What each screen is, and what to check
  • Site day home — Home screen for each person (switch 'Viewing as' at the top): Levels & tests, Visits & inspections, Daily work log.
  • Lab test sheets — Field density, Proctor, CBR, WMM sieve analysis and concrete cubes. Type readings in the white boxes; the app calculates, draws the graph and shows Pass/Fail. 'Print view' shows the sheet to sign.
  • Level sheet — Levels for a stretch: enter RLs or staff readings (BS / IS / FS), compare with design, cross-section and cut/fill quantity. To be changed to level-book style entry with variable points and toe.
  • RFIs — Raise and send RFIs, attach tests and levels, record full or part approval (the rest becomes an '-A' re-inspection RFI), and the approved-quantity summary per layer.

Please note: wrong or missing fields, wording site staff won't understand, tests or formats we use that are missing, and how the department/consultant actually approves RFIs on your sites.
