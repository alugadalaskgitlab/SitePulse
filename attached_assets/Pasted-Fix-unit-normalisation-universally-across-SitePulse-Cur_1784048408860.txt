Fix unit normalisation universally across SitePulse.

Current defect:
Imported BOQ units such as:
- 1 Cum
- 1.00 Cum
- 1 Sqm
- 1 Hect
are still being shown in operational screens even after earlier normalisation changes.

This means some modules are still reading the raw imported unit instead of the canonical unit.

1. Audit every place in the repo where BOQ or linked operational units are:
   - imported
   - stored
   - normalised
   - compared
   - displayed
   - used in calculations
   - used in sequencing
   - used in recipes
   - used in DPR
   - used in BOM
   - used in Site Requirements
   - used in Material Receipt
   - used in Tomorrow’s Plan
   - used in reports and selectors

2. Create or use one shared canonical unit normalisation function only.

3. Remove harmless leading quantity prefixes from imported unit values.

Examples:
- 1 Cum → Cum
- 1.00 Cum → Cum
- 1 Sqm → Sqm
- 1.00 Sqm → Sqm
- 1 Hect → Hect
- 1.00 Hect → Hect

4. Also normalise common equivalent forms.

Examples:
- Cu.m, Cu M, m3, cubic metre, cubic meter → Cum
- Sq.m, Sq M, m2, square metre, square meter → Sqm
- Hectare, Hectares, Ha → Hect
- Metric Ton, Metric Tonne, Tonne, Ton → MT where applicable
- Number, Numbers, No, Nos → Nos

5. Preserve the original imported unit only for audit/history if required.

6. Use the canonical unit for:
   - all operational display
   - classification
   - recipe matching
   - Auto-Sequence
   - quantity comparisons
   - DPR
   - BOM
   - Site Requirements
   - Material Receipt
   - Tomorrow’s Plan
   - progress and planning screens
   - reports and selectors

7. No operational screen should display:
   - 1 Cum
   - 1 Sqm
   - 1 Hect
   when the canonical unit is available.

8. Fix existing imported projects, not only future imports.

9. Add a safe repeatable backfill/migration for all existing BOQ items and linked records.

10. Do not alter:
   - quantity
   - rate
   - amount
   - conversion factor
   - density
   - resource recipe quantities

11. After backfill:
   - invalidate/refetch affected queries
   - refresh Work Programme
   - refresh BOQ Item Review
   - refresh DPR/BOM/Requirements displays

12. Add tests for:
   - 1 Cum
   - 1.00 Cum
   - Cu.m
   - m3
   - 1 Sqm
   - Sq.m
   - m2
   - 1 Hect
   - Hectare
   - Ha
   - MT
   - Nos
   - mixed case, spacing and punctuation

13. Verify against all existing projects, including NH-167 and Takkadpally-Sirur.

Completion criteria:
- all modules use the same canonical unit
- existing records are corrected
- future imports are corrected automatically
- no screen shows leading quantity prefixes such as “1 Cum”, “1 Sqm” or “1 Hect”
- no BOQ quantity, rate or amount is changed

Do not touch:
- BOQ quantities, rates or amounts
- unit conversion calculations unrelated to harmless text normalisation
- density logic
- resource recipes
- programme dates or bars
- DPR workflow
- Site Requirements approval flow
- permissions
- unrelated modules
