=====================================================================

A5. Remove the expanded-card summary strip (Working Hours / Clock duration / Diesel issued / Tank status) in sectionPresentation. Keep the one-line summary that shows when a card is COLLAPSED.

A7. Work done on: the existing EquipmentActivityAllocationEditor (unchanged behaviour), then a link "+ Split time across another item" (same action as today's "Add Item"), then the "General — not for any one work item (e.g. camp DG, water tanker)" checkbox MOVED here from the top of the card. Remove the sentences "Timed interruptions are separate from the machine-day daily status." and "Saved with this equipment row in the DPR draft. A9. Readability: inside the equipment card and labour rows, field labels become normal-case 12–13px, dark slate (e.g. text-slate-700/800), not 10px uppercase grey. Placeholders slate-500. Numbers shown without 3 decimals: hours 1 decimal ("6.0 h", "1.5 h"), litres whole or 1 decimal.
