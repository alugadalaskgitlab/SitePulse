Do not apportion multi-equipment purchase totals by planned quantities or evenly across machines.

**Why:** Purchase quantities are recorded at requirement-header level; an inferred split would falsely imply evidence about individual machines.

**How to apply:** Attribute only when one identifiable machine accounts for the requirement; otherwise retain an explicitly unattributed amount. Keep purchase-versus-issue warnings informational and inside reporting, never on the raise form.

Daily reporting defines Logged Work as the presence of a usage/DPR equipment entry, not positive hours or positive diesel.

**Why:** This was the requested signal, independent of diesel amount. Since issued diesel comes from those same entries, “Issued but not logged” is not normally reachable from current sources. Do not invent real examples or change the signal to make the flag appear.

**How to apply:** Preserve the agreed first-match status priority. Explain absent real-data examples separately from synthetic status-branch tests.
