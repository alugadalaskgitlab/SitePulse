# INSTRUCTION — VB-VIEW-04

**The Previous Billing Summary belongs to whoever finalises the bill, not only to
whoever may see the company's financial position.**

Baseline: the published commit carrying AUDIT-02 (`05f9f34` or the commit published
from it). State it in your report.

**No calculation changes, no migrations, no new columns.** Every figure on every bill
identical before and after.

---

## Why

On 11-Oct the owner asked for Vendor Financials to be Owner-only by default, covering
"aggregate summaries, vendor statements, Taxes Register and reporting/export APIs" —
recorded in `.agents/memory/vendor-financial-visibility.md`. The Previous Billing
Summary is built from `VendorRunningStatement`, so it went behind that grant.

The owner has now revised that decision, for one specific reason: **whoever finalises
a bill must see what was already deducted on earlier bills**, or an advance or a
diesel recovery gets deducted twice. That is operational information needed to do the
job, not a view of the company's position.

**The revision is limited to this block.** The payables page, the financial summary on
the Vendor Bills list, the Taxes Register and the reporting and export APIs stay
behind View Vendor Financials exactly as they are.

## Part A — Give the block its own data source

**The problem.** In `client/src/pages/VendorBills.tsx` the block renders as
`{canViewFinancials && <VendorRunningStatement bill={bill} bills={financialReview.data || []} … />}`.
Removing the gate alone is not enough: `financialReview` is itself
`enabled: canViewFinancials` and is served by a financial-report endpoint that is
permission-gated on the server. The block would render empty.

**Required.** A narrow endpoint that serves **this one bill's previous-bill context**
and nothing more — for example `GET /api/vendor-bills/:id/previous-bills`:

1. Permission: the same as viewing the bill itself (`vendor_bills` or
   `vendor_bills_view`). **Not** View Vendor Financials.
2. It returns only bills for the **same linked vendor** as the bill in question,
   **earlier** than it by the existing rule in `vendorStatementBills` — unchanged —
   and only within the caller's permitted sites, using the same site scoping the bill
   list already applies.
3. It returns per bill only what the block renders: bill number, bill date, bill
   period, status, net, paid, balance, the running balance the block already computes,
   and that bill's own recorded adjustments and advance deductions.
4. **Voided bills stay excluded**, exactly as now.
5. It is read-only and creates, updates and deletes nothing.

**Constraints.**

- **Do not relax the existing financial-report endpoints.** Do not widen
  `/api/vendor-bills/financial-review`, the taxes register, or any export. This is a
  new, narrower path.
- Do not return aggregate totals across vendors, and do not return any bill belonging
  to a vendor other than this bill's vendor.
- Do not change `vendorStatementBills`, `billReviewAmounts`, `summarizeBillReview`,
  `getBillFinancialTotals` or `billPaymentPosition`.

## Part B — Show the block to whoever can see the bill

**Required.**

1. The Previous Billing Summary renders for any user who can view the bill, fed by
   the Part A endpoint.
2. Everything the block already does stays: the bill period column, the "Running
   balance before this bill" column with its footnote, each bill's adjustments nested
   under it, the scope caveat line, and the exclusion notices naming any bill left out
   and why.
3. A user **with** View Vendor Financials sees exactly what they see today — the same
   figures, from whichever source. Do not produce two different answers for the same
   bill.

**Constraints.**

- The payables page, the Vendor Bills list financial summary, the Taxes Register and
  every report or export stay behind View Vendor Financials. **Confirm each one is
  untouched.**
- Audit history stays Owner and Administrator only, as AUDIT-02 established.
