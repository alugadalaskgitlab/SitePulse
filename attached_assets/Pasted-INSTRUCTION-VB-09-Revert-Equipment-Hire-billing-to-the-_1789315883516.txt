BACKGROUND (confirmed by direct comparison of the codebase before and after the monthly-hire redesign — do not re-derive)

We compared the current build against the last build before the monthly hire feature existed (internally referenced snapshot "sp143", dated 28-Aug). In that version, Equipment Hire bills were created through the exact same unified flow as every other bill type: pick vendor + period, a banner reads "Equipment usage for [VENDOR] ([period]) from DPR & Plant records" with a "PULL N ITEMS" button, each pulled item becomes an editable line item (date, category badge, description, qty, unit, rate, amount), admins can also ADD ITEM manually, and one GST card at the bottom applies a rate per category actually present in the bill (including "GST ON EQUIPMENT"), computed off that category's subtotal. This never depended on Equipment Master's hire fields being complete — it billed off whatever was actually logged.

Confirmed: this shared infrastructure was NEVER deleted. It is still fully intact and working today for Material/Transport/Labour/Other. What happened is Equipment was surgically carved OUT of it when the monthly-hire redesign shipped:
- client/src/pages/VendorBills.tsx line 2768: the "PULL N ITEMS" banner condition now reads `billType !== "other" && billType !== "equipment"` — Equipment is explicitly excluded from the shared banner/pull mechanism.
- Lines 787-795: vendor discovery routes billType==="equipment" to a separate `/api/vendor-bills/equipment-hire-discovery` endpoint (eligibility-gated on complete Equipment Master hire fields) instead of the shared `/api/vendor-bills/discover-vendors` endpoint every other bill type still uses.
- Lines ~2355-2768 (the current build's numbering may have shifted slightly — verify against the live file): a separate `data-testid="equipment-hire-straight-form"` Card renders instead, built around selecting one piece of equipment from a dropdown fed only by hire-eligible equipment, auto-filling its stored hire terms, and a per-group diesel recovery calculator. A duplicate, already-dead entry point for the same system sits behind `{false && ...}` around line 2355 — this can now be deleted outright rather than kept as dead code.
- Line 3196: a separate single-field GST branch (`singleType = billType === "equipment" ? ...`) exists alongside the multi-category GST card that already correctly handles "GST ON EQUIPMENT" for every other bill type (line 3190).

This is why the vendor-discovery bug we found (video-traced, JCB hired by NARASIMHULU showing "No hired equipment is linked to this vendor for the selected period" despite genuine billable activity) exists: the new path requires complete Equipment Master hire terms (billing basis, rate, start date) before it will show equipment as billable at all, and gives no explanation when that data is incomplete. The old shared path never had this requirement. Reverting removes this entire class of bug, not just this one instance.
