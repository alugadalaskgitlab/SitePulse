# VB-CALENDAR-SUMMARY-01 — Complete Vendor Bill Review & Financial Visibility

**Objective:** Improve the existing Vendor Bills module in one focused implementation, covering three requirements:

1. Restore the Activity / Breakdown Calendar in saved Equipment Hire bills.
2. Add filter-responsive payment summaries to the Vendor Bills list.
3. Add a read-only, vendor-wise running statement.

Reuse existing components, billing calculations, payment records and financial safeguards. Do not redesign Vendor Bills.

## PART A — Saved Equipment Hire Activity Calendar

**Existing problem:** The Activity / Breakdown Calendar works while preparing monthly equipment-hire bills, but is unavailable or shows no information after saving as Draft, Verified, Approved or Paid.

Review the existing calendar components and Vendor Bill Detail rendering.

Requirements:

- Show an expandable **View Activity / Breakdown Calendar** for each applicable equipment item in saved Vendor Bills.
- Support Draft, Verified, Approved and Paid bills.
- Include date-wise working activity, recorded hours/trips, breakdown, no-work days, diesel where applicable, hire deductions, decisions and period totals.
- Reuse the existing preparation-calendar presentation wherever practical.
- Use frozen historical evidence and billing decisions for approved/paid bills, not silently recalculated current activity.
- For legacy bills without complete saved evidence, show available read-only information and clearly identify unavailable details.
- Fix nonfunctional existing calendar buttons.
- Preserve existing Bill PDF/Excel and independent Calendar export formats.

## PART B — Filter-Responsive Vendor Bill Totals

Add a compact financial summary above the existing Vendor Bills list.

The totals must respond to all currently selected filters: status, vendor, date range, site and bill type/category.

Show:

- Number of bills
- Gross amount
- Deductions/adjustments
- Net bill value
- Recorded payments
- Positive outstanding balances
- Negative balances/vendor credits separately
- Approved and unpaid amount eligible for payment

When no status filter is selected, include all permitted bills in the financial overview, but do not classify Draft, Verified or Paid bills as approved outstanding payable.

Use the existing authoritative bill financial totals and payment fields.

Never simply offset one vendor's negative bills against another vendor's payable amount.

Keep summary figures consistent with the bill amounts displayed below.

## PART C — Read-Only Vendor Running Statement

In Vendor Bill Detail, provide an expandable **Vendor Statement**.

Show chronological information, where recorded, for:

- Previous bills
- Current bill
- Bill statuses
- Existing recorded payments
- Verified advances and adjustments
- Outstanding amounts
- Negative net balances and available credits

Use confirmed Vendor Master identity where available.

For one-time/free-text vendors, do not merge parties solely because names are similar.

**Important:** This statement is initially informational, not an automatic accounting ledger.

Show missing historical opening balances and payments as **Not established** rather than assuming zero.

Distinguish actual recorded transactions from calculated balances and provisional amounts.

Do not automatically carry forward, offset or consume previous advances.

## PART D — Existing Financial Records Must Remain Unchanged

Do not change:

- Vendor Bill amounts, line items, rates or GST calculations
- Existing advances, payments or adjustments
- Paid, approved or verified status
- Hire calculation snapshots
- VM-VB-01 vendor identity logic
- DPRs or equipment usage
- Stock, diesel, procurement or accounting records

No migration or historical financial backfill unless an essential additive change is identified and explicitly reported for approval.

Do not implement Tally export or automatic advance settlement in this batch.

## PART E — Focused Verification

Test:

1. Saved monthly-hire calendar in Draft, Verified, Approved and Paid bills.
2. Multiple equipment items and breakdown/idle days.
3. Legacy bills with incomplete calendar evidence.
4. Status-filtered and unfiltered financial summaries.
5. Partially paid and fully paid bills.
6. Negative bills and advance deductions.
7. Different vendors and site-scoped visibility.
8. Linked and temporary/free-text vendors.
9. Preservation of all existing financial records.

Run focused tests and one production build only. Avoid full-suite testing, unrelated refactoring and repeated investigations.

Deliver one concise report containing:
- What was corrected
- Test count and build result
- Any unresolved limitation
- Publication readiness

**Do not publish automatically. STOP.**
