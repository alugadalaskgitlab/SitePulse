PURPOSE
Sometimes the contractor does not want to revise item-wise rates (BC/DBM/Prime/Tack), but instead offers one consolidated service/scope rate, usually per MT, for the selected HLC scope. Example: HLC does only mixing / plant-side deployment / selected scope items, and the contractor supplies materials. In this case profitability must be able to calculate revenue from a scope-based rate rather than from item-wise rates.

IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS

3) AUTO-CONVERT ₹/MT AND ₹/CUM
Both Scope Rate ₹/MT and Scope Rate ₹/CUM should be shown.

Behavior:
- If user enters/edit Scope Rate ₹/MT, auto-calculate Scope Rate ₹/CUM
- If user enters/edit Scope Rate ₹/CUM, auto-calculate Scope Rate ₹/MT
- The last edited field becomes the driver

Conversion basis:
Use the current site-level ratio:
MT per CUM = Total Site MT / Total Site CUM

Then:
₹/CUM = ₹/MT × (MT per CUM)
₹/MT = ₹/CUM ÷ (MT per CUM)

If Total Site CUM is zero or invalid, handle safely and do not break typing.

4) REVENUE CALCULATION IN SCOPE RATE MODE
When Revenue Mode = Scope Rate:

If the active basis is MT:
Revenue = (Scope Rate ₹/MT × Total Site MT) + Adjustment Revenue

If the active basis is CUM:
Revenue = (Scope Rate ₹/CUM × Total Site CUM) + Adjustment Revenue

This Revenue should then be used in profitability calculations:
Profit = Revenue - In-Scope Cost
Margin % = Profit / Revenue

When scenario is active:
- Revenue should remain based on entered scope rate + adjustment
- Cost side may change according to scenario
- Profit and margin should update from revised in-scope cost as already designed

- It must NOT affect technical costing
- It must NOT affect procurement
- It must NOT alter estimator/job estimator values
- It must NOT rewrite item rates
