# UX-SIMPLE-01 — Master-Based Entry, Trip Linking and Equipment Ownership

**Objective:** Simplify SitePulse's existing Material Trip entry/editing and Equipment Hire Vendor Bill workflow into a practical **Search → Select → Save** experience.

Users should not need to understand internal IDs, identity links, audit versions, fingerprints or separate confirmation processes simply to record or correct a trip.

**This is one consolidated, focused task covering:**

1. Simple Material Trip Add/Edit.
2. Searchable selections exclusively from the respective Masters.
3. Automatic Vendor/Equipment association for billing.
4. Correct Owned/Hired equipment identification in Vendor Bills.

Reuse the existing functionality developed under TRIP-VB-INTEGRATION-01, VB-TIPPER-03 and VB-HIRE-TERMS-01. Do not rebuild these features or create parallel workflows.

## 1. One Simple Material Trip Form

Use the existing Add/Edit Material Trip form for both new and historical entries.

The normal form should clearly show:

**Material Details**
- Material Source: Vendor Supply / Own Source
- Material Supplier or Own Source description
- Material, Quantity and Unit
- Site and Date

**Transportation Details**
- Transporter
- Vehicle / Tipper
- Existing trip particulars

Preserve the existing BOQ/work context where applicable, without creating another separate review process.

Use the normal existing **Edit** action for permitted users.

The user should complete the required selections and click **Save** once.

Avoid separate *Set Roles*, *Review Links*, *Confirm Vendor Identity* and *Hire Billing Link* steps in the ordinary workflow.

Keep exceptional historical correction mechanisms available internally where necessary.

## 2. Vendor Master — Searchable Dropdowns

Material Supplier and Transporter must use **Vendor Master exclusively** for normal searchable suggestions.

Requirements:

- Search by entering two or more characters.
- Match characters appearing anywhere in the Master Name or Business Name.
- Search should be case-insensitive.
- Display **Business Name** prominently.
- If Business Name is blank, display the Master Name.
- Where necessary, show both names to distinguish similar vendors.
- Save the selected **Vendor Master ID**, not merely the displayed text.
- Use only active Vendor Master records.

**Example:**

Typing `SARA` should show:

SARAVANA METAL INDUSTRIES

Typing `NARSI` should show the relevant Vendor Master parties containing that text.

Do not mix historical free-text names, previously entered trip names, locally remembered suppliers or cached alternatives into the dropdown results.

Selecting a Master should establish the existing supported vendor association automatically.

Do not create duplicate Vendor Master entries.

### Own Source

Where material comes from HLC's own source:

- Select **Own Source**.
- Enter the borrow-area/source description.
- Do not require a material supplier.
- Still allow a separate hired transporter and vehicle.

The material supplier and transporter must remain independent identities.

## 3. Equipment Master — Searchable Vehicle Dropdown

Vehicle/Tipper selection must come from **Equipment Master exclusively** for normal suggestions.

Search by any part of the registration number, including the last four digits.

**Example:**

Typing `4470` should display matching Equipment Master vehicles such as:

Tipper · TS15UE4470 · Hired · Gangaram Narsimulu

Display:

- Equipment Type
- Registration Number
- Ownership: Owned / Hired
- Owner/Vendor Business Name when hired

Save the selected **Equipment Master ID**.

For hired equipment, resolve the owner through its confirmed Vendor Master association wherever available.

Do not use historical trip vehicle numbers, locally cached vehicle lists or manually typed registrations as normal dropdown suggestions.

If several registrations match, display all matching Master records for the user to select.

Do not automatically create duplicate equipment records.

## 4. Automatic Owner and Transporter Association

When a hired vehicle is selected:

- Identify its confirmed Equipment Master owner.
- Suggest the corresponding Vendor Master transporter.
- If the transporter has not yet been selected, prefill the suggested Master party where the association is unambiguous.
- If the selected transporter differs from the confirmed equipment owner, show one short, understandable warning.

Do not silently change an already selected transporter.

A genuine difference may be valid, so preserve the ability for an authorised user to review it.

For normal unambiguous selections, saving the form should automatically establish the existing vendor/equipment billing association.

Use the current backend ID validation and audit mechanisms internally.

**No separate confirmation button, technical review screen or mandatory verification reason should be required for routine valid selections.**

## 5. Historical Trips — Simple Correction

For existing trips, including those where supplier, transporter and vehicle were originally typed manually:

- Open the ordinary Edit form.
- Display the existing recorded information.
- If a Master association already exists, preselect the correct Master.
- If unlinked, show the old value as a legacy recorded value, clearly distinguished from a verified Master selection.
- Allow the authorised user to search and select the correct Vendor Master and Equipment Master records.
- Save all selected associations in one operation.

Preserve original historical values and changes through the existing audit/history mechanisms.

Do not silently rewrite submitted or protected transactional evidence.

For an actual identity conflict, locked record or previously billed vehicle/date, retain existing safeguards and show one concise explanation of the required authorised action.

Do not bypass permissions, approved correction controls or duplicate-billing checks.

The user must not have to repeat the same confirmation every time the form is reopened.

## 6. Explicit Outside-Vendor / Outside-Vehicle Exception

Normal suggestions must come only from Masters.

For a genuine one-time external vendor or unregistered vehicle, provide a clearly separated:

**Other / Not in Master**

option, subject to existing permissions.

This allows exceptional free-text entry without polluting Master dropdowns.

Such entries must remain distinguishable from Master-linked records.

Do not automatically create Vendor Master or Equipment Master entries.

Where an outside vehicle cannot be linked to a registered equipment record, do not pretend its Hire Billing Link is confirmed.

The existing permitted one-time billing workflow must remain available where applicable.

## 7. Simplify the Visible Interface

Remove unnecessary clutter from normal trip Add/Edit:

- Technical internal IDs
- Audit fingerprints and version numbers
- Large explanatory paragraphs
- Multiple role/link confirmation buttons
- Repeated identity warnings
- Mandatory reasons for ordinary valid selections

Replace separate routine link actions with one normal **Edit → Save** workflow.

Keep audit details accessible through existing History/Admin functionality.

Use clear, consistent field labels, capitalisation and concise validation messages.

Ensure dropdown search is keyboard-friendly and practical on smaller screens.

Do not add another full-screen review interface.

## 8. Correct Equipment Ownership in Vendor Bills

Some equipment appearing in **Equipment Hire Vendor Bills** is displaying **Owned**, although the equipment may actually be hired.

Trace ownership and equipment identity through:

Equipment Master → Vendor Bill equipment selection → Hire calculation → Saved Bill Detail → PDF/Excel.

Determine whether the issue is caused by:

- Incorrect Equipment Master matching
- Wrong selected Equipment Master ID
- Incorrect ownership display logic
- A genuine incorrect ownership value in Equipment Master

Use the actual saved Equipment Master ownership field as the authoritative source.

Display:

**Hired — Vendor Business Name**

for hired equipment with a confirmed vendor.

Display:

**Owned — HLC**

for HLC-owned equipment.

Do not infer ownership from an equipment type, vehicle number fragment, free-text name or billing basis.

If a saved Equipment Master record genuinely says Owned, do not automatically change it to Hired. Report the discrepancy for authorised correction.

### Important billing safeguard

HLC-owned equipment must not automatically generate an outside vendor's equipment-hire liability.

Investigate the current Auto-Generated Monthly Hire selection to ensure only genuinely eligible hired equipment is used for vendor-payable hire bills.

Preserve legitimate operational usage of HLC-owned equipment.

Do not alter existing bills or historical financial calculations merely to correct a displayed label.

## 9. Keep Hire Basis Independent of Ownership

Ownership and Hire Basis are different concepts.

An equipment item may belong to one vendor but be billed on different bases for different assignments.

Preserve the existing Vendor Bill selection of:

- Trip
- Daily
- Hourly
- Monthly

Equipment Master values remain default suggestions only.

The actual agreed basis, rate and period must be selected and preserved in the Vendor Bill.

Do not introduce Hire Basis or Hire Rate into the Material Trip form.

Do not alter the existing Vendor Bill Rate Card structure in this batch.

## 10. Vendor Bill — Automatic Trip Discovery

Once a Material Trip has the correct saved Vendor Master and Equipment Master associations, it must become available as supporting evidence for the relevant Equipment Hire Vendor Bill without another routine linking operation.

Preserve the current billing rules:

**Trip Hire**
- Count eligible confirmed trips individually.
- Do not include already billed trips again.

**Daily Hire**
- Combine eligible activity by vehicle/date.
- Multiple trips on the same date count as one vehicle/day.
- Retain Full/Half/Excluded decisions and reasons.

**Hourly Hire**
- Use actual recorded working hours only.
- Never infer hours from trip count.

**Monthly Hire**
- Preserve the existing monthly calculation, calendar and contractual terms.

The selected Hire Basis in the Vendor Bill governs charging, regardless of Equipment Master's default basis.

Keep the existing server-side cross-basis duplicate-billing protection.

Do not create duplicate Equipment Usage records from Material Trips.

## 11. Permissions and Historical Safety

Respect the existing permission system.

- Permitted site staff can create and edit ordinary trips within their authorised scope.
- Read-only users cannot edit.
- Master maintenance remains restricted to authorised users.
- Exceptional historical identity conflicts must follow existing authorised correction safeguards.
- Vendor Bill financial rates, amounts, payments and approvals remain protected by existing financial permissions.

Do not change existing approvals, DPR correction rules or Vendor Bill financial access.

Preserve submitted DPRs, Material Receipts, inventory, diesel records, paid bills and accounting data.

Never silently overwrite an already billed or protected trip identity.

## 13. Strict Scope — Do Not Touch

Do not modify:

- Historical Vendor Bill amounts or payment records
- Vendor Bill financial overview, GST/TDS reports or Excel exports
- Submitted DPR calculations
- Diesel or inventory accounting
- Purchase Indents, Purchase Orders or Material Receipts
- Payroll or employee records
- Tally integration
- Vendor Bill Rate Card architecture
- Unrelated app menus, permissions or modules

Do not perform database cloning, synchronisation, production-data backfills or automatic master-data corrections.

Avoid unnecessary schema changes. If one is unavoidable, explain the exact requirement and production impact before proceeding.

**Do not publish automatically.**
