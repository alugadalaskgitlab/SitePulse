## Item 1 — The monthly table is under the wrong heading, and renders twice

**Problem.** `client/src/components/vendor-bills/VendorBillReview.tsx` renders the
heading `Previous Billing Summary` and then, immediately beneath it, the **current
bill's** `HireMonthlyReview`. The heading describes the vendor's account *before*
this bill; the content under it is *this* bill. On screen the two are
indistinguishable.

The same `HireMonthlyReview` is then rendered again further down the page by
`client/src/components/vendor-bills/SavedBillCalendars.tsx`, so the user sees the
identical Monthly Billing Summary table, and its identical footnotes, twice.

**Required.**

- Remove the `HireMonthlyReview` render from inside the `Previous Billing Summary`
  section. That section must contain only previous-bill information.
- Keep exactly **one** Monthly Billing Summary per equipment on the page, in the
  Equipment Hire section where the rest of this bill's detail lives.
- Do not delete `HireMonthlyReview`, `projectHireReview` or
  `shared/vendorBillLayout.ts`. This is a placement fix.

## Item 2 — Previous billing should not appear when there is no previous billing

**Problem.** When a vendor has no earlier bill, the section still renders a
four-box grid reading `—  —  —  —`, plus a paragraph explaining what the dashes
mean. Four empty boxes and an explanation of their emptiness.

`shared/vendorBillReview.ts` already returns nulls throughout when there is no
confirmed vendor or no earlier bill, so the condition is available.

**Required.**

- When there are **no previous bills**, replace the whole block with one short
  line, in the same spirit as the existing unlinked-vendor line
  (*"Unlinked/free-text vendor: previous balances are unavailable..."*) — for
  example *"No previous bills for this vendor."* Do not render the empty grid and
  do not render the explanatory paragraph.
- When there **are** previous bills, show them: the bills with their dates, status
  and net value, and the balance carried up to this bill. The existing
  `View Previous Bills` expandable already lists them — surface the latest bill
  and the carried balance without requiring the user to expand it.
- Keep the scope caveat (confirmed vendor · permitted sites · excludes this and
  later bills) but shorten it to one line, and show it only when there is
  something to caveat.

**`Last Payment` must be resolved, not left as it is.** It is currently
hardcoded to `—` and can never display a value, because the application records
the moment a status was set to paid, not a payment date or amount. Either remove
the field, or label it honestly as not recorded. **Do not** substitute the
paid-status timestamp for a payment date, and do not infer a last payment amount
from a cumulative paid figure.

## Item 3 — The same days are shown three times; keep one table

**Problem.** Three components present the same saved daily evidence for the same
bill at three depths:

1. **Activity Calendar** — inside `HireMonthlyReview`
   (`client/src/components/vendor-bills/HireMonthlyReview.tsx`): date plus one
   classification word, built from `snapshot.workingSheet`.
2. **Historical working sheet** — `HistoricalHireWorkingSheet` in
   `client/src/pages/VendorBills.tsx` (rendered at the per-statement block inside
   the Equipment Hire Calculation Snapshot card): ten columns from the same
   `snapshot.workingSheet`.
3. **Detailed recorded evidence** — `SavedBillCalendars.tsx`, via
   `EquipmentHireDailyTable`: the same days with readings, hours, trips, diesel,
   consumption and status.

None of these is previous-bill information. All three describe the bill on screen.

**Required.** Keep **one** day-by-day table per equipment and remove the other two
renders.

**The one that remains must not lose information.** Before removing anything,
compare the columns. The historical working sheet carries at least four things the
detailed table does not: **expected diesel**, the **recorded decision** per day,
**remarks**, and **movement references**. Carry those into the surviving table
rather than dropping them.

**Verify on a legacy bill specifically.** `Detailed recorded evidence` only renders
its full table when `hasFrozenDailyRows` is true; otherwise it degrades to
*"Complete frozen daily activity is unavailable for this legacy bill."* The
historical working sheet reads `snapshot.workingSheet` directly and can still show
rows in that case. **Do not leave legacy bills with less detail than they have
today.** If the surviving table cannot cover a legacy bill, say so and stop rather
than removing the sheet that can.

## Item 4 — Cut the explanatory text, keep the one sentence that carries information

**Remove** these, on screen and in the exports:

- *"Days Billed follows the hire terms, not Worked Days. Gross is before
  separately recorded deductions."*
- *"Recorded evidence only. Missing activity is not Idle or Breakdown."*
- The paragraph explaining what a dash means in the previous-billing grid (Item 2
  removes the grid anyway when it is empty).

**Keep, and make more prominent rather than less:**

> *"September 2026: 11 dates require review."*

This is not boilerplate. On the bill that prompted this batch the table reads
**Days Billed 30, Worked Days 19, Idle 0, Breakdown 0** — nineteen days have
recorded activity and eleven do not, inside a month that is being billed in full.
That sentence is the application stating what it does not know, and it is the most
useful line on the page. Keep its wording factual and keep the count exact. Do not
soften it, and do not imply those days were idle or breakdown.

Keep the *"Monthly allocation / payable day-equivalent is unavailable from saved
evidence"* note, since it appears only when figures are genuinely blank and
explains why the row is empty.
