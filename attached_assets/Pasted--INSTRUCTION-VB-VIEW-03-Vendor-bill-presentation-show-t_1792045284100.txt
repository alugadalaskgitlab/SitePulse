## Part A — A wrong column is formatted as money (fix this first, it is live)

In `client/src/components/vendor-bills/HireMonthlyReview.tsx`, the Monthly Billing
Summary body renders:

```tsx
{j === 6 && typeof cell === "number" ? money(cell) : cell}
```

When VB-HIRE-06 split the breakdown column into two, a column was inserted and this
index was not moved. `MONTHLY_BILL_HEADERS` is now:

```
0 Sl. No. · 1 Bill Period · 2 Days Billed · 3 Worked Days ·
4 Idle Days (Work Not Available) · 5 Breakdown Days ·
6 Operator / Driver Unavailable Days · 7 Gross Amount (₹)
```

So index 6 is the **Operator / Driver Unavailable Days** count, which renders as
`₹0.00`, and **Gross Amount** renders as a bare `130000` with no symbol and no
decimals. Both are visible on bills `HLC/VB/2026/0054` and `HLC/VB/2026/0058` right
now.

**Required.** Format the Gross Amount column as money and the day-count columns as
plain integers. Do not key the formatter on a hard-coded index again — derive it
from the header list or from the column's meaning, so the next column added does
not break it.

## Part B — The totals block names every deduction

**Problem.** On the saved Bill Detail the totals block jumps from the item total
straight to TDS. On `HLC/VB/2026/0054` the ₹2,000 breakdown deduction appears only
inside the calculation snapshot in small grey text, and the ₹58,000 shown as TOTAL
is already net of it, so the ₹60,000 gross never appears at all. A vendor reading
this cannot see what came off or why.

**Required.** On screen and in both exports, for draft, verified, approved and paid
bills alike, present the money as a sequence of named rows:

1. **Gross** — the amount before anything is deducted.
2. **One row per kind of deduction or credit**, each carrying its reason beneath
   the label in smaller text: the dates, the recorded reason, and the decision
   (full day / half day / manual).
   - breakdown deductions from the hire statement
   - operator / driver unavailable deductions
   - diesel recovery
   - advance deduction
   - each additional deduction / credit, with its free-text reason
3. **GST rows**, where a bill carries GST — additions shown as additions.
4. **IT TDS**.
5. **Net payable**, last, with a rule above it and set larger than the rest.

**Constraints.**

- **Hide any row with no value.** No `₹0.00` rows and no row for a deduction that
  did not happen. A bill with one deduction shows four lines: gross, that
  deduction, TDS, net.
- **The statement-level deduction must get its own row.** Today only
  `additionalAdjustments` are rendered as rows; a hire-statement deduction is
  folded into the item amount with no line. That is the specific omission being
  fixed.
- **Do not change the net.** The rows must sum to the net the bill already stores.
  If they cannot be made to sum without a calculation change, stop and report it
  rather than adjusting a figure.
- Deductions in red, additions in green, as now.

## Part C — Previous bills carry their period, their own adjustments, and a name that means something

**Three faults in the Previous Billing Summary.**

1. The table has a bill date but **no bill period**. The user remembers the month
   worked, not the day the bill was typed.
2. The column header **"Calculated cumulative movement*"** in
   `client/src/components/vendor-bills/VendorBillReview.tsx` does not say what the
   figure is.
3. The **"Recorded adjustments / advance deductions"** block in that same file
   lists every past bill's adjustments together, separately from the bills, so
   reading one bill's history means scrolling between two places.

**Required.**

- Add **Bill period** beside the bill date, as `from to` dates.
- Rename the cumulative column to **"Running balance before this bill"**, keep its
  arithmetic exactly as it is, and keep its footnote.
- **Nest each bill's adjustments under that bill** and remove the separate block.
  Where a bill has none, show one short line saying so rather than leaving it
  blank.
- Keep the scope caveat (confirmed vendor · permitted sites · excludes this and
  later bills) as one line.

Do not change `shared/vendorBillReview.ts` arithmetic. This is placement and
labelling.

## Part E — The exported PDF opens with the bill, not with an empty grid

**Problem.** Page 1 of the `NAFEEZ 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30` export is a 22-column
table of which **16 columns are empty on every row**, carrying three rows of
content, followed by half a page of white space. The company name is set large on
every page above four dense lines of small text. The equipment type — the thing
being paid for — appears only inside a description string.

**Required — page 1 is the abstract.**

```
High Lane Constructions Pvt Ltd

Vendor · Equipment · Hire basis and rate · Bill no. · Bill date · Period

Summary of hire charges for the period <from> to <to>
  [ monthly summary table ]
  [ the named totals rows from Part B ]
```

- **Name the equipment type** — Tractor Dozer, Soil Compactor, Excavator — as its
  own field, from the equipment master, not parsed out of a description.
- Include the **Not Recorded** count as its own column in the monthly summary, so
  the unaccounted days appear on the bill and not only in an on-screen banner.
- Page 1 must stand alone as the document a vendor signs. Daily detail starts on
  page 2.

**Required — the running header on later pages.** One line: bill number, vendor,
equipment, period, page number. Not the full company block.

**Required — the daily detail table.**

- **Drop every column that is empty for all rows in the period** before rendering,
  on page 1 and in the detail.
- Size the remaining columns to their content and fit the table to the page width.
  No date may wrap to two lines while a wide column sits half empty.
- Readable type with real row padding — roughly 8pt, not the present ~6pt.
- **Strip the edit provenance from Project / Site.** The column still prints
  `TAKKADPALLY-SIRUR - Edited by Admin - 2026-10-10 05:59:34`. VB-HIRE-06 applied
  `getBaseSiteName` for fresh calculations; the exports must apply the same helper
  on render so saved bills print the base site name too. **Display only — do not
  alter any stored DPR site string or any version history.**

Apply all of this to both the server PDF (`server/routes.ts`) and the client PDF
and Excel exports.

## Part F — Say why a monthly machine was left out

**Problem.** A monthly hire machine seeds into the New Vendor Bill form only when
its equipment-master hire dates cover the requested period. Nafeez's Soil Compactor
had a hire end date of 30-Sep-2026, so for a 01–05 October period it was silently
excluded and the form showed only
`NO BILLABLE ACTIVITIES AVAILABLE`. The user spent an hour on a form that had
correctly decided there was nothing to bill and never said so.

**Required.** Where a vendor's monthly hire equipment is excluded by the seeding
conditions in `client/src/pages/VendorBills.tsx`, name each machine and the reason:

- `Soil Compactor — excluded: hire recorded as ended 30-Sep-2026`
- `Tipper — excluded: no hire start date recorded`
- `JCB — excluded: no hire rate recorded`
- `Excavator — excluded: custom monthly divisor not set`

**Constraints.** Do not relax or change any seeding condition. Do not auto-correct
a hire date. This is a notice only — the user fixes the master record themselves.
