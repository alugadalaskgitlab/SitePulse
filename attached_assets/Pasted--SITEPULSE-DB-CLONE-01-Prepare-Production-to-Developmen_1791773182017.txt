**SITEPULSE-DB-CLONE-01 — Prepare Production-to-Development Database Clone**

We want the Development/Preview environment to contain an exact working copy of the Live/Production SitePulse data, so we can test historical trips, Vendor Bills, masters and other workflows before deployment.

This must be a **one-way replacement**, not a merge or ongoing sync.

Please investigate and report:

1. Confirm that Production and Development use physically separate databases. Identify their environments and database identifiers without exposing credentials.
2. Check database engine, schema versions, migrations, tables and constraints for compatibility.
3. Identify any production attachments, bill images, trip documents or other files held outside the database that are required for a usable development copy.
4. Identify risks involving users, permissions, sessions, external services, notifications, storage and production credentials.
5. Propose a verified backup of Production and a separate backup of Development before replacing Development.
6. Explain how to replace Development data completely with the Production snapshot, preserve relationships and sequence counters, and verify record counts and integrity.
7. Ensure Development remains isolated after cloning, cannot write to Production, and cannot trigger real payments, messages or external business operations.
8. Explain whether existing Development login credentials or test users would be affected, and how authorised testing access would be retained.
9. Provide the exact execution plan, estimated downtime, rollback procedure and any limitations.

**Strict safety requirements:**
- Production is a read-only source throughout this operation.
- No production schema or data changes.
- No automatic deployment.
- No merge or partial copying that leaves inconsistent records.
- Never expose database credentials or sensitive production data in the report.
- Do not run migrations against Production.
- Do not begin restoring or replacing either database yet.

**STOP after investigation and report. Wait for explicit approval before executing the clone.**
