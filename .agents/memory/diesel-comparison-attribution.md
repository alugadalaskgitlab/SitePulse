---
name: Diesel comparison attribution
description: Conservative equipment purchase attribution and report-only gap warnings
---

Do not apportion multi-equipment purchase totals by planned quantities or evenly across machines.

**Why:** Purchase quantities are recorded at requirement-header level; an inferred split would falsely imply evidence about individual machines.

**How to apply:** Attribute only when one identifiable machine accounts for the requirement; otherwise retain an explicitly unattributed amount. Keep purchase-versus-issue warnings informational and inside reporting, never on the raise form.
