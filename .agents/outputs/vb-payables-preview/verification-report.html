Approved behavior

  • Vendor, date range and optional site filter; five category blocks, excluded counts and hire calendars.
  • Editable unsaved GST inputs, latest accessible vendor/category saved-bill suggestions labelled “from last bill — check”, blank amber missing rates. Explicit zero is preserved; unknown never silently becomes zero.
  • Pre-tax is primary, followed by GST (indicative) and With GST. Missing commercial rates produce incomplete totals rather than invented prices.
  • Unallocated availability/maintenance is separate, with reasons, excluded from the site subtotal and included once in the authorized vendor total. Restricted users cannot obtain unallocated financial details through a site-access bypass.
  • Generate again after input changes; exports disabled until current. Each download revalidates permissions and scope with a fresh protected GET.

No business writes

The real service and both export builders ran against development data with PostgreSQL default_transaction_read_only=on. No migrations or application auth bypass were added. Full-row content checksums include status columns. Initial baseline, pre/post export and final snapshots agree.
