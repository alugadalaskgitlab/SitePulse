Observed issues:
1. Excavation for foundation of bridges/structures is still being divided into road Reach 1/2/3/4.
2. Strip seal expansion joints are showing both road reach bars and structure/location bars.
3. Bridge numbering / enamel painting is being planned as road reach work.
4. MS railing / bridge railing is also being planned as road reach work.
5. Similar issue may apply to bearings, tar paper, POT/PTFE bearings, drainage spouts, weepholes, approach slab, filter media behind abutment, expansion joints, crash barrier on bridges, etc.

Root issue:
The app seems to clean/skip structure bars only when:
- planningWorkType = structure, or
- the item already has source = structure_import bars.

That is not enough.

- Auto-generate
