PART C — TRANSPORT RATE CARD: LEAD DISTANCE + PAYLOAD
=====================================================================
Regional practice: transport of mixes is paid ₹ per km, charged two-way, per truck, with a standard payload (normally 30 MT). A single "RATE" box cannot express that — which is why most TRANSPORT rows on the Vendor Rate Cards screen sit at 0.00.
C1. On the VENDOR RATE CARDS screen (the TRANSPORT block), each row gets a small "RATE SETUP" control opening a dialog with THREE inputs:
    - Rate (₹ per km per load)
    - Lead distance — ONE WAY (km)   [label must say one way; the system doubles it]
    - Payload (MT)  — default 30, editable per row
    And THREE read-only computed lines, recalculating as the user types:
    - Two-way distance = lead × 2 (km)
    - ₹ per trip = rate × lead × 2
    - ₹ per MT = (rate × lead × 2) ÷ payload
C2. Use the estimator's formula exactly: perMT = distance * 2 * rate / payload (client/src/lib/mixCalc.ts ~271). Extract it into a shared helper so the estimator and the rate card cannot drift apart; the estimator's behaviour must not change.
C3. SAVE ALL THREE INPUTS, not only the derived figure — rate, one-way lead distance and payload are each stored on the rate-card row, so months later the basis of a rate is visible.
C4. Guards: payload must be > 0 (a 0 payload shows "Payload required for ₹/MT" and leaves ₹/MT blank — it never divides by zero and never shows 0). Lead distance 0 or blank → ₹ per trip and ₹ per MT both blank, and the row keeps whatever flat rate was typed.
C5. The TRANSPORT row then displays, in the list, "₹950/km · 12 km one way · 30 MT → ₹22,800/trip · ₹760/MT" (compact, one line) instead of a bare rate box. Keep the existing UNIT dropdown; when the three fields are filled the unit is derived and shown read-only.
C6. SCHEMA: this needs new nullable columns on the vendor rate-card row (lead_distance_km real NULL, payload_mt real NULL, rate_per_km real NULL — name them to match existing conventions). ADDITIVE ONLY: no column may be dropped, renamed, made NOT NULL or given a default that rewrites existing rows. Existing rate-card rows keep working untouched with the new columns NULL. Show the Publish preview SQL before anything is published.
C7. Do not touch the EQUIPMENT, MATERIALS or LABOUR blocks of the rate card.

=====================================================================
PART D — THOSE RATES FLOW INTO THE PULLED ITEMS
=====================================================================
D1. When transport items are pulled into a bill for a vendor whose rate card has the three fields set, each transport row picks up that rate automatically and prices itself.
D2. Pricing basis — DEFAULT IS PER TRIP:
    - A trip-based item prices at ₹ per trip (rate × lead × 2), and shows the actual MT carried beside it as INFORMATION only.
    - A weight-based item (the receipt carries a weight) prices at ₹ per MT × actual MT.
    - Both bases are visible on the row: "₹22,800/trip (₹950/km × 12 km × 2) · carried 28.4 MT" — so nothing is a black box.
D3. The user may override the basis ON THE ROW (a small per-trip / per-MT toggle). The override applies to that row only and is never written back to the rate card.
D4. Where the row's own lead distance differs from the rate card's (the trip record has its own leadDistance), use the ROW's distance and show "lead 14 km (card 12 km)" in amber so the difference is visible. Never silently pick one.
D5. Existing transport rows on saved bills keep their stored amounts. Do not recalculate or overwrite any saved bill.
D6. calcCandidateAmount in shared/vendorBillCandidates.ts already does leadDistance * 2 * rate — extend it for the payload/per-MT case rather than adding a parallel function, and keep its current behaviour identical when payload is NULL.
