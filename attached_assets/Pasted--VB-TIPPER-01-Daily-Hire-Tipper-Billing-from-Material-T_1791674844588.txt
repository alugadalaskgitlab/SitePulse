# VB-TIPPER-01 — Daily-Hire Tipper Billing from Material Trip Register

**Objective:** Enable SitePulse to prepare Equipment Hire Vendor Bills for daily-hired tippers using trips already recorded in Site Operations → Material Movement / Trip Register, without requiring duplicate equipment logs in submitted DPRs.

## 1. Reuse existing records

The current trip register stores vehicleNumber, supplier, materialSourceType, transportType, site, date and quantity.

Equipment Master stores registrationNumber, ownership, vendorName, hireBillingBasis and hireRate.

Existing Vendor Bill hire logic already supports DPR, equipment usage and material-trip activities.

Reuse that infrastructure. Do not build a separate billing system.

## 2. Match hired tippers with Equipment Master

For historical trips:

- Match normalized registration numbers against Equipment Master.
- Check the transport vendor/owner against the hired-equipment vendor.
- Only suggest matches with a unique, credible identity.
- Flag missing registrations, conflicting owners and multiple equipment matches for manual review.
- Never silently assign an uncertain vehicle or vendor.

Provide a simple confirmation mechanism so an authorised user can link historical trips to the correct Equipment Master vehicle for billing.

Preserve the original trip, vehicle number, supplier, receipt, material source and quantities.

For future trips, allow selecting an existing hired vehicle from Equipment Master while retaining free-text entry for unregistered vehicles.

Do not treat Own Source material as company-owned transport.

## 3. Daily-hire billing

For equipment configured with Daily Hire terms:

- Collect the confirmed trip dates for the selected vehicle, vendor and billing period.
- Count each equipment/date only once, regardless of number of trips.
- Use the applicable hire terms and daily rate from Equipment Master.
- Allow reviewer-approved full-day, half-day or excluded-day decisions, with reasons.
- Display the trip counts as supporting evidence, not as the hire-charge multiplier.
- Do not infer missing clock times, meter readings, diesel consumption or working hours from trip counts.

## 4. Vendor Bill integration

In New Vendor Bill → Equipment Hire:

- Include confirmed historical material-trip working days alongside existing eligible DPR/equipment usage.
- Show vehicle, date, number of trips, hire days, rate and amount.
- Provide links to underlying trip records.
- Prevent double billing where the same tipper/date is already represented by DPR or equipment usage.
- Check existing bills and previously billed activity.
- Preserve existing monthly, hourly and trip-rate billing logic.

Importantly, do not accidentally bill the material itself when the purpose is only hiring the vehicle.

## 5. Historical and financial safeguards

Do not backfill DPR equipment logs, generate fake equipment usage, rewrite material-trip history, or alter existing vendor bills.

Any new trip-to-equipment association must be traceable and reviewable.

Do not change stock, diesel, material receipts, Equipment Master hire rates or paid accounting records.

## 6. Verification

Test:
- One hired tipper making several trips on one date: one billable day.
- Same tipper working on multiple dates.
- Multiple tippers belonging to one vendor.
- Historical manually entered vehicle numbers.
- Incorrect/ambiguous vehicle or vendor matches.
- A date already appearing in DPR equipment logs.
- Existing billed/paid activity.
- Own Source material with agency-hired transport.
- Future trip entry with Equipment Master selection.

Use focused tests and one production build. Avoid broad refactoring, full-suite testing and unrelated modules.

Provide one concise report explaining how historical trip matching is confirmed, how daily-hire lines reach Vendor Bills, whether a schema change is required, and the verification result.

Do not publish automatically or modify production records during development. STOP.
