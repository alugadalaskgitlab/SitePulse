Fix the Resource Review false flags affecting valid earthwork recipes.

Current defect:
Imported BOQ units may contain a quantity prefix such as “1 Cum”. The Resource Review unit normalisation converts this to “1CUM”, but the earthwork classifier checks only for an exact “CUM” match. As a result, valid embankment, borrow-earth, subgrade and earthen-shoulder items are classified as “other”, and their valid water tanker is incorrectly flagged as “Water tanker on a other item”.

1. Correct the unit normalisation used by Resource Review so these equivalent values are recognised as CUM:
   - Cum
   - 1 Cum
   - 1.00 Cum
   - Cu.m
   - Cu M
   - m3
   - cubic metre / cubic meter
   - similar harmless spacing, punctuation and case variations

2. Do not make earthwork classification depend only on an exact unit match.

3. Classify an item as earthwork when its description clearly contains recognised earthwork terms such as:
   - roadway excavation
   - earthwork excavation
   - embankment
   - borrowed earth / borrow earth / borrow pits
   - selected soil
   - subgrade constructed with soil or approved earth
   - earthen shoulders
   - earth filling
   - filling with excavated earth

4. Use all available reliable context when classifying:
   - full description
   - normalised unit
   - layerType
   - persisted Work Category/category name, where available

5. Where the persisted Work Category clearly identifies Earthwork, Subgrade or Earthen Shoulders, do not classify the item as “other” merely because the unit format is unusual.

6. Keep description matching case-insensitive and tolerant of punctuation, spacing and common wording variations.

7. A water tanker must be treated as a valid resource for:
   - excavation where watering is applicable
   - embankment
   - borrow-earth filling
   - soil subgrade
   - earthen shoulders
   - GSB/WMM and other pavement layers
   - concrete where applicable

8. After correcting classification, the four items presently shown in Resource Review must no longer be flagged merely for having a water tanker:
   - embankment with excavated roadway earth
   - embankment with borrowed earth
   - subgrade with approved borrow material
   - earthen shoulders with selected soil

9. Do not remove the water tanker or alter the recipes of these items as part of this fix.

10. Resource Review may continue reviewing BOQ items whether or not they are presently included in the Work Program, but its classification and warning message must be technically correct.

11. Improve the warning grammar so future messages say:
   - “Water tanker on an item classified as ‘Other’”
   instead of:
   - “Water tanker on a other item”

12. Add focused tests covering:
   - unit “Cum”
   - unit “1 Cum”
   - embankment descriptions
   - borrow-earth descriptions
   - soil subgrade descriptions
   - earthen-shoulder descriptions
   - legitimate water tanker usage
   - genuinely unrelated “Other” items that should still be flagged

Do not touch:
- the actual equipment, labour or material recipes
- Work Program scheduling or sequencing
- BOQ category mapping covered by Instruction 005
- DPR or BOM flows
- BOQ quantities, rates or amounts
- unrelated Resource Review anomaly rules
- auto-clean behaviour for genuinely incorrect heavy equipment
