Two places I'd push back or sharpen before anyone builds anything.

First, "side defaults to full width when hidden" needs care. For most contractors that's a safe default. For you, it isn't — you told me directly that LHS/RHS split execution is your normal way of working, not the exception. If the simplified form quietly defaults to full width and tucks side away behind an "advanced" toggle, it'll be wrong for most of your actual projects. Their own preset-button idea ("LHS first, then RHS") actually fixes this on its own — if side is chosen up front via a preset rather than typed into a row, it stops being an "advanced" field and becomes the main decision. I'd just make sure that's explicit before it's built, so nobody quietly implements "hide side, default to full width" as a separate, contradicting shortcut.

Second, the "confidence rule" — let the app act automatically when it's sure, tag it "auto," let you correct it — is the right pattern for suggestions: which item, which side, which stage. It is the wrong pattern for anything we deliberately made hard to undo: deleting a bar that has DPR records against it, overriding a quantity that exceeds the BOQ, reassigning a side that conflicts with existing site records. Those guardrails exist on purpose because a wrong auto-guess there causes real damage — lost records, wrong material orders. So "auto-act with a correction tag" should apply to guesses, never to anything currently protected by an explicit confirmation. Worth stating that boundary out loud before it gets built, so it doesn't get generalized further than it should.
