**Stop the app answering for the user, then give him one place to correct what it
already answered.**

Baseline: the published commit carrying VB-EXPORT-01 (`4a23e3d` or the commit

**No calculation changes. One additive migration, nullable, no backfill. No new

## Why

Of 57 bills, **51 carry no site**. That is not forgetfulness. In
`client/src/pages/VendorBills.tsx:616` the site selector is initialised to `"all"`:

```tsx
const [selectedSiteId, setSelectedSiteId] = useState<string>("all");
```

and on save, `"all"` becomes `siteId: null` (line 2274). The schema comment at
`shared/schema.ts:2362` says null means *"the bill deliberately spans all sites"* —
so the app has been recording a deliberate claim that nobody made.

The same shape appears in payments: **36 of 38 bills at status `paid` carry no paid
amount**, ₹9,44,683.98 of them. A status asserting the bill is settled, with no
figure behind it.

Both are the same fault: **the app states something the user never said.**

## Part A — The form stops answering for the user

**Required.**

1. The site selector opens **unselected**, showing a prompt such as "Select site".
   Remove the `"all"` initial value.
2. **ALL SITES stays available** as a deliberate choice. It is a real answer for a
   bill that genuinely spans sites; it is only wrong as a default.
3. A bill cannot be saved with no answer. The refusal names the field in plain words.
4. The same for payment: **a bill cannot be moved to `paid` without an amount.** The
   amount may be zero if that is the truth, but zero must be typed, not assumed.
5. Editing an existing bill that has no site does **not** force a site to be chosen in
   order to save some other change. It shows the gap and offers to fill it. We are not
   making old work unopenable.

**Constraints.** Do not change the server's permission checks. Do not change
`siteId`'s meaning — null still means all sites. Do not change the status rules.

---

## Part B — Record that ALL SITES was actually chosen

**The problem.** Once Part A ships, a null site will mean two different things: the
user chose ALL SITES, or the bill predates this batch. Without separating them, the
correction list in Part C can never empty, because the bills that are correct look
identical to the bills that are not.

**Required.** One additive, nullable column on `vendorBills` recording that the site
answer was given deliberately — for example `siteScopeConfirmed boolean`, null by
default.

- New bills set it when saved, whichever answer was chosen.
- **Existing rows are not backfilled.** Null continues to mean "we do not know".
- A bill with a site, or with ALL SITES confirmed, is complete. A bill with neither is
  incomplete.

**Constraints.** Nullable. No default applied to existing rows. No other table
touched.

## Part C — One list of bills needing attention

**Required.** A page, reachable from the Vendor Bills area, listing every bill with
something unanswered. One row per bill, showing the bill number, date, vendor, net,
status, and **what specifically is missing**:

- no site and no confirmed ALL SITES answer;
- status `paid` with no recorded paid amount;
- no billed period recorded;
- bill dated **before** the end of its own billed period;
- a negative net.

Each row has a count summary at the top — how many bills, how many of each kind of
gap — and the list can be filtered to one kind.

**Correcting from the list.**

1. A row can be corrected in place: set the site, or confirm ALL SITES, or record the
   paid amount, or set the period.
2. **One bill at a time, on an explicit click.** No select-all, no bulk apply. These
   are judgements, not a cleanup.
3. Every correction writes an audit entry through the existing `recordChange` helper,
   recording the field, the old value, the new value and the actor.
4. Correcting the site or the period **must not change any amount, any status, any
   frozen snapshot, or any payment record.** Recording a paid amount changes the paid
   figure and nothing else — it does not alter the net, and does not move the status.
5. A corrected bill leaves the list. Nothing is ever removed from the list by any
   other means.

**Permission.** Administrator or Owner only. Do not add a permission key; use the
existing administrator check.

## Part D — Suggest a site only where the data supports one

**Required.** For a bill carrying hire statements, the statement knows the equipment
and the period. Where that equipment's recorded activity falls on **exactly one
site** across the statement's dates, the row may show that site as a **suggestion**,
labelled as a suggestion, with the evidence named ("JCB-02 worked only at
Takkadpally-Sirur between 1 and 4 October").

**Constraints — read these carefully.**

- **A suggestion is never applied automatically.** The user clicks to accept it.
- Where the equipment worked on more than one site in the period, or on none,
  **show no suggestion**. Do not pick the most frequent. Do not pick the first.
- Do not suggest anything for bills without hire statements in this batch. There are
  45 of those and nothing on the bill points at a site; guessing from trips or DPRs
  is a separate question we will take separately.
- The suggestion is computed on read. Nothing is stored.
