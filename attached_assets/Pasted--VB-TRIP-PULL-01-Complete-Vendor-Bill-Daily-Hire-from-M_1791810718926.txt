# VB-TRIP-PULL-01 — Complete Vendor Bill Daily Hire from Material Trips

**Objective:** Correct Equipment Hire Vendor Bills so hired tippers recorded in Material Trips are discovered and billed even when they have no DPR Equipment Log entry for those dates.

This is a focused end-to-end correction. Do not redesign Material Trips or add a Hire Type field to the Trip form.

## 1. Trace the Actual Trip-to-Bill Flow

Review the existing implementation:

Material Trip → Selected Equipment Master ID → Confirmed Transporter/Vendor ID → Saved Hire Billing Evidence → Vendor Bill Equipment Hire.

Find why a correctly saved, master-linked historical trip is not appearing in the Vendor Bill hire activity list.

Check the complete data retrieval and filtering path, including the existing trip hire confirmation, vendor matching, equipment eligibility and activity aggregation.

Do not assume a DPR equipment entry exists.

## 2. Material Trips Must Be an Independent Activity Source

A correctly linked, eligible Material Trip must contribute trip evidence without a DPR Equipment Log or Equipment Usage record.

For a hired vehicle with a valid Equipment Master selection and verified matching vendor:

- Use its persisted trip and Master associations.
- Reuse or complete the existing internal hire-confirmation evidence.
- Make it discoverable in the Vendor Bill for the selected vendor and date period.
- Do not require any new role/link confirmation for an already valid saved selection.

Do not automatically validate ambiguous free-text records or mismatched owners.

When evidence is missing or invalid, show a concise, specific exclusion reason in the Vendor Bill.

## 3. Vendor Bill — Correct Eligibility and Charging

When the user selects the vendor and billing period, eligible hired tippers should appear even if they have **trip activity only**.

The user then selects the actual Hire Basis:

**Daily Hire**
- Group activity by Equipment Master vehicle and date.
- Multiple trips on the same date count as one potential hire day.
- Preserve Full/Half/Excluded decisions and their reasons.
- Calculate gross hire using agreed daily rate × payable days.

**Trip Hire**
- Count individually eligible confirmed trips.
- Apply the bill-specific rate per trip.

**Hourly Hire**
- Require actual recorded working hours.
- Do not convert trip counts into hours.

**Monthly Hire**
- Retain the existing monthly calculation and evidence rules.

Where DPR/equipment activity and Material Trips coexist, show the supporting evidence without duplicate charges.

Preserve existing cross-basis duplicate-billing safeguards.

## 4. Vendor and Equipment Identity

Use the actual persisted Vendor Master and Equipment Master IDs.

Use Vendor Master Business Name for display, with the existing Master Name as fallback.

Do not incorrectly reject a vehicle because its original free-text owner spelling differs from the confirmed Master name.

For genuine ownership or transporter conflicts, preserve authorised review rather than silently reassigning the vehicle.

Do not modify equipment ownership, hire defaults or past trip records.

## 5. Test the Exact Failure

Create isolated test fixtures representing:

1. A hired tipper with multiple Material Trips but **zero DPR Equipment Logs and zero Equipment Usage records**.
2. Correctly selected Equipment Master vehicle and confirmed matching transporter/vendor.
3. Equipment Master default Trip Hire, while the current Vendor Bill uses Daily Hire.
4. Two or more trips on one date and additional trips on another date.
5. The tipper appearing under the correct vendor and selected billing period.
6. Daily Hire counting each payable vehicle/date once.
7. Trip Hire counting each individually eligible trip.
8. Saving and reopening the Draft preserving the trip evidence and agreed hire terms.
9. A genuine owner mismatch remaining excluded with a clear reason.
10. A previously billed date being blocked from duplicate charging.

Test the actual route and save/reload path, not calculation helpers alone.

Where possible, test the signed-in Vendor Bill screen. Do not change real historical records merely to perform the test.

## 6. Strict Safeguards

Do not touch unrelated:
- DPRs or equipment logs
- Material Trip quantities, dates or receipts
- Equipment or Vendor Master records
- Vendor Bill financial overview, tax calculations or payment status
- Procurement, inventory, diesel or Tally integration

Do not create artificial DPR usage records to make trips billable.

Use focused tests and one production build. Do not run the full suite.

Do not publish automatically or perform production-data changes.

## 7. Report

Explain:

- The precise cause of the missing trip-only tippers.
- The code correction.
- How the user should select and bill them.
- Focused test results and build result.
- Whether any migration is required.
- Remaining limitations, if any.

**STOP after verification.**
