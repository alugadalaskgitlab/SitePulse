---
name: Legacy liquid tank assignment
description: Why an unassigned fuel balance can offset a tank deficit without indicating missing fuel
---

Historical LDO/bitumen receipts could be recorded without a tank, while subsequent use was deducted from Tank 1. An offsetting tank deficit and unassigned balance do not, by themselves, indicate missing physical fuel.

**Why:** The Owner distinguished correcting this historical classification from receiving or consuming fuel. Moving the unassigned balance to a tank must leave the party's total stock, physical readings and operational history intact.

**How to apply:** Treat reviewed legacy tank assignments as position-only corrections, not new receipts, issues, consumption or financial events. Do not repair historical receipts or infer new physical movements from these offsets.
