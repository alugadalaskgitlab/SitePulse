# TRIP-VB-INTEGRATION-01 — Consolidate Material Trip Roles, Vendor/Equipment Links and Hire Billing

**Objective:** Complete the existing Material Trip → Vendor Master → Equipment Master → Vendor Bill workflow so site staff can identify the material source, transporter and actual vehicle in one clear form, without repeated linking or duplicate records.

This is one consolidated, focused integration task. Reuse the existing components, APIs, audit confirmations, eligibility calculations and VB-TIPPER-03 / VB-HIRE-TERMS-01 functionality.

Do not build parallel masters, separate billing screens or a new rate-card system.

## 1. Material Trip — Clear Roles

In the existing Material Trip form and historical **Set / Change Roles** dialog, retain the distinction:

**Material Source**
- Vendor-supplied material → select the material supplier from Vendor Master.
- Own Source → select Own Source and enter the source/borrow-area description.

**Transport**
- Same vendor transports the material.
- Different transporter brings the material.
- Own vehicle transports the material.

When selecting an existing material supplier or transporter, use an actual Vendor Master dropdown/search and save its ID alongside the recorded name.

For an existing historical free-text name, display the previously recorded value and suggest an appropriate master match without automatically changing the record.

Allow authorised confirmation after reviewing the actual party.

Do not assume the material supplier and transporter are the same merely because both appear on one trip.

## 2. Vehicle Selection — Link Equipment Master

Add an Equipment Master vehicle selector to the trip roles form when transport is by an agency-hired vehicle.

Display:
- Registration number
- Equipment type
- Recorded owner
- Confirmed Vendor Master identity, where available

Allow search by registration number.

If the registration matches exactly one Equipment Master vehicle, suggest it.

If multiple matches exist, require manual selection.

For a genuine outside vehicle not registered in Equipment Master, retain manual registration entry. Do not automatically create another Equipment Master record.

For HLC's own vehicle, reuse the existing internal equipment selection.

After an authorised confirmation, preserve the existing trip registration number and save the confirmed equipment association through the existing audited Hire Billing Link mechanism.

Do not silently replace an existing confirmed equipment identity.

## 3. Combine Role and Hire-Link Review

The existing **Set / Change Roles** and **Hire Billing Link** buttons perform related functions.

Do not remove their underlying capabilities.

Provide one understandable **Trip Details / Review Links** action that presents:

- Material Source
- Material Supplier
- Transporter
- Vehicle Registration
- Equipment Master Match
- Equipment Owner / Vendor Master Match
- Hire Billing Link Status

Use the existing role-editing and audited hire-confirmation operations within this workflow.

Do not require the user to open multiple dialogs just to complete one trip.

Where information has already been confirmed, show the saved result instead of repeatedly asking for confirmation.

Show clear states such as:
- Roles Pending
- Roles Confirmed
- Vehicle Link Pending
- Vehicle Linked
- Review Required

A user may reopen a completed trip to inspect the details, but must not be prompted to confirm it again unless something relevant has actually changed.

## 4. Historical Trips — Preserve Original Records

Existing trip data must remain safe.

Do not rewrite:
- Trip date
- Vehicle registration
- Original transporter text
- Material and quantity
- Receipt number
- Site and source information
- Submitted DPR information

Where an association is missing, permit authorised confirmation of the correct Vendor Master and Equipment Master IDs.

Store the confirmation separately or through existing supported foreign keys and audit evidence.

If saved role information genuinely requires correction, use the existing authorised edit flow with audit history.

A historical link should become invalid only when a relevant identity or supporting record has actually changed.

Reopening or refreshing a trip must not cause an already valid link to disappear.

## 5. Vendor Master Linking — Remove Repetitive Confusion

In **Vendor Master → Link Vendors**, display two views:

**Pending Links** — default; only genuinely unlinked or invalid associations.

**Confirmed Links** — previously confirmed records, searchable by vendor and vehicle registration.

For Equipment Master records:
- Show registration number prominently.
- Display confirmed Vendor Master name.
- Display confirmation date and user.
- Do not repeatedly show a successfully confirmed equipment row as pending.

Several vehicles may belong to one Vendor Master party.

Different recorded spellings may be linked to the same reviewed party through authorised confirmation.

Do not automatically merge vendors based on similar names.

Ensure the Pending/Confirmed status reflects persisted database evidence after a refresh or a new login.

## 6. Hire Basis and Rates

Keep hire terms independent of equipment identity.

Equipment Master retains default rates and hire bases.

Vendor Bill selects the actual agreed basis for that bill:
- Trip
- Daily
- Hourly
- Monthly

Preserve VB-HIRE-TERMS-01's per-bill editable rate and period.

Do not require changes to Equipment Master merely because the current assignment uses a different hire basis.

The existing Vendor Bill Rate Card may be used to suggest applicable vendor/vehicle rates **only where it already supports that agreement and those terms**.

Do not assume a general transport rate card is automatically the correct equipment hire rate.

If the Rate Card does not support multiple hire bases, do not force its structure to change in this batch. Retain direct bill-specific rate entry.

A future rate-card extension can be considered separately.

## 7. Vendor Bill Integration

When preparing **New Vendor Bill → Equipment Hire**:

- Select the vendor.
- Select the eligible linked equipment.
- Choose actual hire basis, rate and billing period.
- Pull relevant confirmed Material Trips and DPR/equipment activity.
- Review supported payable quantities.
- Save the draft with its agreed terms and supporting evidence.

For Daily Hire:
- Multiple trips on one date count as one vehicle/date.
- Full/Half/Excluded decisions remain explicit.
- Show supporting trip evidence.
- Preserve server-side duplicate billing protection.

For Trip Hire:
- Use eligible individual trips without duplicating the same charged trip.

For Hourly Hire:
- Use recorded hours only.

For Monthly Hire:
- Continue the existing monthly hire calculation.

Do not infer working hours from trip counts or treat every confirmed trip as automatically payable.

## 8. Permissions

Apply existing permissions consistently.

- Site staff may record trips and view their permitted operational information.
- Only users with relevant edit/confirmation rights may change roles or confirm equipment links.
- Vendor Master identity confirmation requires the existing Administrator/Owner authority.
- Vendor Bill financial details, rates and approval actions remain restricted to users with financial permissions.
- Do not expose private rates, payments or bills to staff merely because they can view a trip.

## 9. Verification — One Realistic End-to-End Workflow

Test a historical case structured like Trip #737:

Material vendor: SARAVANA METAL INDUSTRIES  
Transporter: NARSIMULU  
Vehicle: TS15UE4470

Do not assume the actual database's parties or vehicle registration are correctly matched without reviewing their persisted IDs.

Verify:

1. Material supplier is linked to its own Vendor Master.
2. Transporter is linked to the correct Vendor Master.
3. Vehicle is linked to the correct existing Equipment Master.
4. Equipment owner identity remains independently verified.
5. Previously confirmed links survive refresh and reopening.
6. Pending rows disappear from Pending Links after successful confirmation.
7. Confirmed links remain visible under Confirmed Links.
8. Same vendor owning several tippers is supported.
9. Own Source with a different hired transporter works.
10. One-time free-text transporter and vehicle remain supported.
11. A confirmed historical trip becomes discoverable for Vendor Bill preparation.
12. Daily Hire calculates one vehicle/date despite multiple trips.
13. Trip Hire counts chargeable individual trips.
14. Existing billed activity cannot be billed again under another hire basis.
15. Saving and reopening the draft preserves terms and evidence.
16. Existing submitted DPRs, material receipts, inventory and historical bills remain unchanged.

Use focused automated tests and one production build.

Where feasible, inspect the signed-in operational workflow. If that is unavailable, report it clearly rather than claiming visual verification.

## 10. Scope and Completion

Do not touch unrelated procurement, diesel, payroll, stock, Tally integration, the Vendor Bills financial overview or historical payment data.

Avoid destructive database migrations, unnecessary new tables, duplicate UI controls and wholesale component rewrites.

If a schema change is genuinely necessary, identify it and report its production implications before publication.

Do not publish automatically.

Provide one concise completion report with:
- Root causes
- Changes made
- Exact user navigation for linking an existing trip
- Any remaining limitations
- Focused test results
- Production build result
- SQL/migration requirements

**STOP after implementation and verification.**
