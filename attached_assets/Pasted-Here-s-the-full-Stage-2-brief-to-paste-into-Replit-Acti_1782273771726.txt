54|## 4. Per-km quote (per-site `perkm` mode)

56|When a site is in **Per km** mode:
57|```js
58|// Our cost per km, from the jobs in that stretch:
59|var stretchKm = site.totalRoadKm;                  // from road length
60|var ourCostForStretch = Σ over used mixes: computeRate(mix,{transPerMT:lead}).perMT_cost * mixMT_in_stretch
61|                        + primeCost + tackCost;     // per current Quote Mode + materialsBy
62|var ourCostPerKm = stretchKm>0 ? ourCostForStretch / stretchKm : 0;
63|```
64|Show three labelled numbers: **Our cost ₹/km** · **Contract ₹/km** (their offer / your ask) · **Profit ₹/km** = Contract − Our cost, plus **Total profit** = profit/km × km. Apply the per-km adjustment as today. Add the **"includes:" chip** so it's clear whether materials/transport/laying are in the per-km figure.
