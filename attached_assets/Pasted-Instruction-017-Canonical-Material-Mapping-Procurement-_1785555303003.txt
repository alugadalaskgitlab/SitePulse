Instruction 017 — Canonical Material Mapping, Procurement Horizon and Honest Actionable Shortfall

Purpose

Correct the Work Demand Procurement tab so that:

1. BOM materials are matched to stock and procurement records through stable material IDs, not names.
2. Unmapped materials are blocked from IRN/PI action instead of being treated as zero stock.
3. Procurement recommendations are based on demand up to a selected planning horizon, not automatically on the entire Work Programme.
4. Confirmed incoming includes only real operational commitments, not merely requirements, allocations or unapproved PI/IRN requests.
5. The screen describes the present stock model honestly. SitePulse currently has company/custody-level stock, not a reliable physical stock ledger by Site/HMP/RMC/Store location.
6. The quantity passed into the existing Material Requirement flow is the actionable quantity for the selected horizon.

Expected files

- shared/planningEngine.ts
- server/routes.ts
- server/storage.ts
- client/src/pages/WorkDemand.tsx

Touch shared/schema.ts only if a small additive mapping field or index is genuinely required after inspecting the existing schema.

DO NOT TOUCH OR REDESIGN

- material_requirements
- material_requirement_allocations
- requirementId/allocationId architecture
- Instruction 016B destination-confirmation flow
- allocation permission and authorisation flow
- PI/IRN transactional linkage
- submitPurchaserAction
- placeOrderIndent
- recordDelivery
- derivePiStatus
- checkAndCompleteIndent
- storesVerifyIrn and issue-recording business logic
- createDraftGrnFromPi
- GRN finalisation
- Admin override
- PI-linked GRN skip-search logic
- cancelStoreGrn
- manual PI/IRN creation
- Tomorrow’s Plan integration
- Immediate Requirement integration
- Site Material Received integration
- Stores Hub redesign
- physical stock-location ledger

This instruction changes only Work Demand procurement intelligence, material resolution, table presentation and the suggested quantity passed into the existing requirement flow.

1. Define the demand quantities clearly

For every BOM material row, calculate and return:

- totalProgrammeDemand
- demandUpToSelectedDate
- futureRequirement

Definitions:

totalProgrammeDemand:
- total material demand across the full programmed Work Programme horizon.

demandUpToSelectedDate:
- cumulative programmed material demand due up to the selected procurement horizon.

futureRequirement:
- max(0, totalProgrammeDemand - demandUpToSelectedDate).

For BOQ items with Work Programme bars:

- derive demand from the programmed quantities distributed across the programmed periods.

For BOQ items without programme bars:

- retain the existing full-BOQ fallback only for Total Programme Demand;
- flag the row:
  “Not programmed — full BOQ quantity shown for planning only”;
- do not automatically include the entire unprogrammed BOQ quantity in immediate/actionable procurement;
- include it in actionable demand only when the user deliberately selects Entire Programme.

Do not use totalProgrammeDemand as the default Raise PI/IRN quantity.

2. Add a Procurement Horizon selector

Add a visible selector above the Procurement table:

- Up to Current Programme Month
- Next 30 Days
- Next Programme Month
- Custom Date
- Entire Programme

Default:

- Up to Current Programme Month

Rules:

Up to Current Programme Month:
- include cumulative demand through the current Work Programme month.

Next 30 Days:
- include cumulative demand through today + 30 days based on the programme schedule.

Next Programme Month:
- include cumulative demand through the end of the next programme month.

Custom Date:
- require a date;
- include cumulative demand through that date.

Entire Programme:
- include the entire programmed demand;
- show a warning:
  “Entire Programme selected — recommendations may include materials required several months later.”

Changing the horizon must recalculate:

- demandUpToSelectedDate
- confirmed incoming relevant to that demand
- actionable shortfall
- future requirement
- recommended action
- quantity passed into Raise IRN/PI/Split.

3. Use canonical material IDs from demand generation onward

Every BOM procurement row must carry a stable material identity.

For the live Work Demand/BOM path, use:

- materialType = "plant_material"
- canonical plant_materials.id

Also carry:

- sourceBoqItemId
- canonical material name
- displayed BOM material description
- UOM
- requiredByDate
- procurement route
- programme-month breakdown

Do not resolve stock after the fact by matching displayed material names.

The hardcoded/fallback material categories currently generated in planningEngine.ts, including examples such as:

- GSB Material
- WMM Material / WMM Processed
- aggregates
- Bitumen VG-30
- bitumen emulsion
- filler
- fuel/process fuel

must resolve through a stable material master mapping, code, category or stored BOQ/BOM mapping.

Do not depend on an exact typed label in plant_materials.name.

4. Mark unresolved mappings explicitly

Where a BOM material cannot be resolved to a canonical material ID:

Return:

- materialMappingUnresolved = true
- materialId = null
- a clear reason where practical

In the Procurement table show:

- “Material mapping required”
- action:
  “Resolve Material Mapping”

Do not show:

- Raise PI
- Raise IRN
- Review Split
- Current/HLC stock as zero
- confirmed incoming as zero as though verified

Use “—” for quantities that cannot be reliably calculated because the material is unresolved.

Do not silently create a new material master record from the BOM description.

5. Provide a focused Resolve Material Mapping action

Use an existing BOQ/material mapping interface if one already exists and supports this source.

If no suitable interface exists, add only a small focused dialog that:

- shows the BOM/BOQ material description;
- shows UOM;
- searches existing compatible plant_materials;
- allows an authorised user to select the canonical material;
- saves the stable mapping against the BOQ/BOM source item or existing appropriate mapping entity;
- does not create a duplicate material automatically.

After mapping:

- refresh the affected Procurement row;
- recalculate stock, incoming and recommendation;
- do not require a full browser reload.

If there is no compatible master item, provide a separate route to the existing material-master management screen. Do not silently create one inside Work Demand.

6. Replace name-based stock matching with materialId matching

The present shortage handler builds stock maps using lower-cased material names even though stock_balances already has materialId.

Replace this with canonical ID matching.

For each mapped BOM row:

- query/aggregate stock using stock_balances.materialId;
- query pending PI/IRN/order records using their stable material identity;
- do not fall back to name matching where a canonical ID exists.

Name matching may be used only as a diagnostic suggestion for unresolved mapping, never as the final stock or procurement join.

7. Present the current stock model honestly

The present schema does not maintain a reliable physical running stock balance by:

- specific Site
- HMP
- RMC
- Central Store location

stock_balances.partyId represents custody/accounting parties, not physical project or plant locations.

Therefore, for this instruction, do not label quantities as:

- Stock at Destination
- Stock at Other Site
- Stock at Plant
- Transferable Site Stock

Instead calculate and show only what the current data can support:

- hlcRecordedStock
- stockWithOtherParties

Definitions:

hlcRecordedStock:
- stock recorded as held by HLC/company under the present custody model.

stockWithOtherParties:
- stock recorded against external parties/subcontractors/vendors under the present reconciliation model.

Show a visible note:

“Current stock figures are company/custody-level balances. Physical stock by Site, HMP, RMC and Store location is not yet maintained separately.”

stockWithOtherParties must be informational only.

Do not automatically treat it as available for internal issue or deduct it from actionable shortage.

8. Calculate confirmed incoming from real commitments only

Do not count the following as confirmed incoming:

- Material Requirement raised
- authorised procurement allocation
- authorised internal allocation
- PI merely raised
- PI awaiting approval
- IRN merely raised
- rejected/cancelled/closed records
- received/completed quantities
- stock merely identified as potentially available

Purchase confirmed incoming should include only real outstanding operational commitments such as:

- Ordered — Awaiting Delivery
- Already Purchased — Awaiting Receipt
- outstanding balance of partially received purchase orders/actions

Internal confirmed incoming should include only a real committed issue/transfer where the existing workflow has reached the actual commitment stage.

Do not count a suggested or merely authorised internal allocation as incoming.

Do not double-count the same quantity between:

- PI approved quantity
- purchaser-action ordered quantity
- purchased quantity
- pending receipt
- partial receipt balance
- received stock
- IRN requested quantity
- internal allocation
- committed issue
- completed issue

Use stable document/item/action IDs and active statuses.

9. Keep planning/request quantities visible but separate

Where useful, expose separately:

- procurementRequestedQty
- internallyAllocatedQty

These are workflow/planning commitments, not confirmed physical incoming.

They may appear in a detail popover or secondary breakdown, but they must not reduce actionableShortfall as confirmed incoming.

The screen must distinguish:

- Requested/authorised
- Operationally ordered/committed
- Physically received/issued

10. Define actionable shortfall using supported data

Calculate:

usableCommittedCoverage =
hlcRecordedStock
+ confirmedIncomingPurchase
+ confirmedInternalIncoming

actionableShortfall =
max(0, demandUpToSelectedDate - usableCommittedCoverage)

futureRequirement =
max(0, totalProgrammeDemand - demandUpToSelectedDate)

Do not subtract stockWithOtherParties automatically.

Because the present system lacks physical location stock, any internal issue suggestion from HLC stock must be advisory and subject to review.

11. Temporary recommendation logic until location-wise stock exists

For a mapped material:

If actionableShortfall <= 0:
- Action = Adequate for Selected Horizon

If actionableShortfall > 0 and hlcRecordedStock <= 0:
- Action = Raise PI
- suggestedProcurementQty = actionableShortfall

If actionableShortfall > 0 and hlcRecordedStock > 0 but less than demandUpToSelectedDate:
- Action = Review HLC Stock + Purchase Balance
- suggestedInternalQty = min(demandUpToSelectedDate, hlcRecordedStock)
- suggestedProcurementQty = actionableShortfall

If HLC recorded stock alone covers demandUpToSelectedDate:
- Action = Review Internal Issue
- do not automatically create an IRN;
- show:
  “HLC recorded stock may cover the selected-period demand — confirm availability and location.”

For unresolved material:
- Action = Resolve Material Mapping

Do not state that stock is available at another site or plant.

Use wording such as:

- “Review available HLC recorded stock”
- “Physical location must be confirmed”
- “Stock with other parties is informational and not automatically available”
- “Internal issue/transfer subject to Stores/PM confirmation”

12. Clarify the IRN recommendation limitation

Until the physical location-stock ledger is implemented, Work Demand cannot reliably determine:

- same-store issue
- plant-to-site transfer
- site-to-site transfer
- exact transferable stock by location

Therefore:

- an IRN recommendation is a review prompt based on HLC recorded stock;
- Stores/PM must confirm the physical availability and source;
- the app must not claim a location-specific transfer automatically.

Do not use stockWithOtherParties as a reason to raise an IRN.

13. Correct the quantity passed into Material Requirement creation

Raise PI:
- pass suggestedProcurementQty for the selected horizon.

Review Internal Issue / Raise IRN:
- pass the reviewed internal quantity for the selected horizon;
- do not automatically pass total programme demand.

Review HLC Stock + Purchase Balance:
- create one Material Requirement for the selected-horizon demand/shortfall;
- show proposed internal review quantity and procurement quantity separately;
- use the existing Instruction 016B allocation flow after authorised review.

The confirmation dialog must show:

- selected procurement horizon
- total programme demand
- demand up to selected date
- HLC recorded stock
- stock with other parties
- confirmed incoming purchase
- confirmed internal incoming
- procurement requested, informational
- internally allocated, informational
- actionable shortfall
- future requirement
- proposed internal quantity
- proposed procurement quantity

Do not prefill a PI for the whole programme unless Entire Programme is deliberately selected.

14. Rebuild the Procurement table using honest columns

Use these columns:

- Material
- Unit
- Total Programme Demand
- Demand to Selected Date
- HLC Recorded Stock
- Stock with Other Parties
- Confirmed Incoming

Confirmed Incoming may show a compact total with a detail popover containing:

Where screen width is limited:

- preserve Material and Action;
- use compact headings with tooltips;

- do not hide essential quantities;
- do not merge total programme demand with immediate actionable demand.

For an unprogrammed BOQ item:

For the current stock model:

“Stock shown here is company/custody-level stock. Physical Site/Plant/Store stock locations are not yet tracked separately.”

For Entire Programme:

“Entire Programme selected — recommendations may include materials required several months later.”

“Informational only — not automatically available for internal issue.”

16. Complete the requiredByDate type correction

ShortageRowResult currently declares requiredByDate, but computeShortageRow does not itself return it consistently and it is added later in the route handler.

Correct this so that:

- computeShortageRow, or the immediate typed result builder responsible for the row, returns requiredByDate directly;
- the type and implementation agree;
- no required property is bolted on through an unsafe later mutation;
- the existing correct runtime date calculation is preserved;
- the TypeScript/build check passes.

17. Complete store_item identity validation readiness

Although the Store Catalogue UI entry point remains deferred, complete server-side identity validation for requirement-linked PI/IRN creation.

For materialType = "plant_material":

- materialId must be present and match the requirement;
- storeItemId must be null.

For materialType = "store_item":

- storeItemId must be present and match the requirement;
- materialId must be null.

Reject:

- mismatched storeItemId
- mismatched materialId
- both populated
- neither populated

Use the existing clear MATERIAL_MISMATCH/identity error handling.

Do not add new Store Catalogue requirement UI in this instruction.

18. Preserve the existing 016B lifecycle

After the corrected recommendation:

- Material Requirement is created through the existing flow;
- status remains awaiting_review;
- destination confirmation remains mandatory;
- allocation authorisation remains permission-gated;
- PI/IRN opens only after authorised allocation;
- no automatic approval;
- no automatic purchase order;
- no automatic internal issue;
- no automatic stock transfer.

This instruction changes only:

- material resolution
- demand horizon
- stock/incoming presentation
- recommendation quantity

19. Error handling

Use the shared API error parser where available.

Do not silently:

- treat an unmapped material as zero stock;
- create a requirement without canonical identity;
- fall back to total programme demand;
- count allocations as confirmed incoming;
- count approved-but-unordered PI quantity as incoming;
- count completed/received quantity as still incoming;
- count stock with other parties as HLC-usable stock;
- claim site/plant location accuracy that the schema does not support;
- fall back to name joins when materialId exists.

Acceptance tests

A. Canonical mapping

- A mapped material uses materialId for stock and pending joins.
- Different display names with the same canonical materialId resolve to one stock identity.
- No exact-name equality is required.
- An unresolved material shows Resolve Material Mapping.
- No Raise PI/IRN appears for unresolved rows.
- No duplicate material master is auto-created.

B. Known fallback materials

Test at least:

- GSB
- WMM
- 10 mm aggregate
- 20 mm aggregate
- Bitumen VG-30
- Bitumen emulsion
- filler

Expected:

- each either resolves to a canonical materialId;
- or clearly shows Material Mapping Required;
- none silently appears as verified zero stock merely because the label differs.

C. Horizon calculation

Use one material programmed over six months.

Confirm:

- Total Programme Demand remains the full six-month quantity.
- Current Programme Month shows cumulative demand only through that month.
- Next 30 Days changes demand correctly.
- Next Programme Month includes the next period.
- Custom Date uses the selected date.
- Entire Programme equals full programmed demand.

D. Near-term versus full programme

Example:

- Total Programme Demand: 6,840 MT
- Demand to Selected Date: 238.6 MT
- HLC Recorded Stock: 0
- Confirmed Incoming: 0

Expected:

- Actionable Shortfall: 238.6 MT
- Raise PI quantity: 238.6 MT
- Future Requirement: 6,601.4 MT
- PI is not prefilled for 6,840 MT.

E. Adequate selected horizon

Example:

- Total Programme Demand: 1,000
- Demand to Selected Date: 200
- HLC Recorded Stock + Confirmed Incoming: 250

Expected:

- Actionable Shortfall: 0
- Future Requirement: 800
- Action: Adequate for Selected Horizon
- No Raise PI because of the later programme demand.

F. HLC recorded stock advisory

Example:

- Demand to Selected Date: 500
- HLC Recorded Stock: 300
- Confirmed Incoming: 0
- Stock with Other Parties: 100

Expected:

- Stock with Other Parties remains informational.
- It is not deducted automatically.
- Actionable Shortfall reflects only supported usable coverage.
- Action says Review HLC Stock + Purchase Balance.
- No claim is made that 300 is located at a particular site or plant.

G. Confirmed purchase incoming

Confirm:

- PI raised but not ordered is excluded.
- PI approved but not ordered is excluded.
- Ordered — Awaiting Delivery is included.
- Already Purchased — Awaiting Receipt is included.
- Partially received order includes only outstanding balance.
- Cancelled purchase is excluded.
- Fully received purchase is excluded from incoming and represented through stock where the existing stock flow posts it.
- No duplicate action is counted twice.

H. Requirement/allocation quantities

Confirm:

- procurementRequestedQty is not counted as confirmed incoming by itself.
- internallyAllocatedQty is not counted as confirmed incoming by itself.
- committed internal issue/transfer is counted only where the existing workflow genuinely records commitment.
- the same IRN/PI quantity is not counted in both requested and confirmed incoming.

I. Unprogrammed item

- Total Programme Demand may show the full BOQ fallback.
- Immediate actionable demand excludes that unscheduled full quantity.
- Warning is visible.
- Entire Programme deliberately includes it.

J. Material Requirement quantity

- Raise PI passes the selected-horizon actionable procurement quantity.
- Internal review/IRN uses the selected-horizon reviewed internal quantity.
- Split uses one selected-horizon requirement with separate suggested internal and procurement portions.
- Full programme quantity is used only under Entire Programme.

K. Required-by date/build

- requiredByDate is returned consistently in the typed shortage result.
- No route-handler mutation is needed to satisfy the required type.
- TypeScript/build check passes.

- Wrong storeItemId is rejected.
