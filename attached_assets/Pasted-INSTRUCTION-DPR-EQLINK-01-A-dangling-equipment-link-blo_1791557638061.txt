THE SYMPTOM
The owner was required by the app to correct overlapping DPR chainages, supplied the
reason the app demanded, and could not save. The screen showed:

    Conflict — Linked equipment usage not found

He changed no equipment on that DPR.

WHERE IT COMES FROM
`server/storage.ts` ~7594, inside `finalizeDprEquipmentUsageTx`:

    for (const log of linkedLogs) {
      const usageId = Number(log.plantUsageId);
      await tx.execute(sql`SELECT id FROM equipment_usage WHERE id = ${usageId} FOR UPDATE`);
      const [usage] = await tx.select().from(equipmentUsage).where(eq(equipmentUsage.id, usageId)).limit(1);
      if (!usage) throw new EquipmentIncomingConflictError("Linked equipment usage not found");

`equipment_logs.plant_usage_id` (`shared/schema.ts` ~242) points at an
`equipment_usage` row that is not there. It surfaces as **HTTP 409** via
`handleEquipmentLifecycleConflict` (`server/routes.ts` ~2558, also ~6051), with a
message naming no equipment, no date and no id.

PART A — INVESTIGATION. NO FEATURE WORK.
=====================================================================
A1. IDENTIFY THE EXACT MISSING REFERENCE. For a reproduction of this failure,
    establish precisely which `equipment_logs` row, which `plant_usage_id`, which
    DPR, date, site and equipment are involved. State them.

A2. WHY DOES A CHAINAGE-ONLY REVISION TOUCH EQUIPMENT AT ALL? This is the central
    question. Observed: `finalizeDprEquipmentUsageTx` is called from the DPR update
    path at `server/storage.ts` ~4192 under `if (isSubmitting)`, with
    `insertedEquipLogs` — i.e. it appears to run on every submit regardless of
    whether any equipment fact changed, over equipment logs that may have been
    re-inserted rather than left alone.
    Determine, with evidence from the code:
      - whether the equipment closure genuinely must run when no equipment field,
        hour, meter, diesel or linkage has changed;
      - whether the equipment logs are deleted and re-inserted on an unrelated
        revision, and if so what that implies for their identity and links;
      - what side effects closure performs (stock, diesel, hire, movement
        successors) and which of them are no-ops when nothing changed.
A3. If the processing is unnecessary for unchanged equipment rows, PROPOSE a narrow
    fix: skip or no-op the closure work for equipment rows whose stored facts are
    unchanged, leaving changed rows fully processed exactly as today. Describe it in
    the report; do not implement it until Part A is reported, and never implement it
    by relaxing a validity check.
A4. SAY WHAT CAUSED THE DANGLING LINK, if the code shows it: a deleted usage, a
    movement successor, a clone, a failed transaction, a migration. Do not fix the
