**UX-SIMPLE-02 — Correct the Material Trip Searchable Dropdowns**

The current UI is not acceptable. It displays two controls per field: a search text box and a separate dropdown. We require **one searchable combobox per field**.

Please correct the existing Material Trip Add/Edit form only.

1. For **Material Supplier, Transporter and Vehicle / Tipper**, use one editable searchable combobox each. As the user types, show matching Master records directly beneath the same input. Clicking a suggestion fills that input and saves the corresponding Master ID.

2. Search Vendor Master using any part of Master Name or Business Name, displaying the Business Name. Search Equipment Master by any part of registration, including the last four digits, displaying **Equipment Type · Registration · Ownership · Vendor Business Name**.

3. Master-only results: no historical free-text names among suggestions. Existing old text may appear as the initial field value, but it must not count as a selected Master record.

4. **Material Supplier must work exactly like Transporter.** Fix its current failure to populate Vendor Master results. For Own Source, show the source-description field instead.

5. Remove the second dropdown, duplicate labels and excessive helper text. Preserve an explicit **Other / Not in Master** exception for authorised one-time entries.

6. Support mouse, keyboard and mobile interaction. Require actual Master selection for ordinary entries, not merely typing a matching name.

7. Preserve the one-Save operation, existing IDs, trip audit history, permissions and billing protections. Do not change billing calculations, Master records or historical bills.

8. Test typing `SARA`, `NARSI` and `4308`, selecting the result, saving, reopening and verifying the saved Master IDs. Visually test the actual signed-in form, including Material Supplier.

Run focused tests and one production build. No migration, no unrelated modifications and no automatic publication. Stop after verification.
