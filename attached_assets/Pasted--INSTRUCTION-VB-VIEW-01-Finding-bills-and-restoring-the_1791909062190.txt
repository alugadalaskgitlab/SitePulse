## Item 1 — Filter bills by the billed period, not the bill date

**Problem.** The Vendor Bills date filter compares `bill.billDate`. A user
searching August 2026 is looking for the bill that *covers* August, not the
bill that was *typed* in August. Observed: bill `HLC/VB/2026/0045` (period
19-Jul to 31-Jul) appears in a 1-Aug-to-30-Sep search, while
`HLC/VB/2026/0058` (period 01-Sep to 30-Sep) does not, because its bill date
is 12-Oct-2026.

**Where.** `shared/vendorBillFinancialReport.ts`, `filterFinancialBills` —
the first two conditions.

**Required.** Match on the bill's period overlapping the requested range:
include a bill when `periodFrom <= dateTo` and `periodTo >= dateFrom`. Fall
back to `billDate` only when a bill has no period recorded, and say so in the
UI help text.

Label the two date inputs so the basis is unambiguous (for example
"Billed period from / to"). If you keep a bill-date search as well, it must be
a separate, clearly labelled control — not the same two boxes behaving
differently.

The same basis must apply to the list, the financial summary, the taxes
summary and every export sheet, since they share this one function.

## Item 2 — Stop the "Unpaid" view hiding bills in silence

**Problem.** The `unpaid` scope drops any bill whose net is negative or that
carries a credit:

```ts
if (filters.scope === "unpaid") {
  const position = billPaymentPosition(bill);
  if (position.net < 0 || position.credit > 0) return false;
  ...
```

Bill `HLC/VB/2026/0056` is a draft August hire bill: gross ₹29,032.26, less a
₹50,000 advance and ₹580.65 TDS, net **₹-21,548.39**. It is invisible in the
default view, with no count, no note and no indication that anything was
withheld. A user concluded the bill did not exist and tried to create it
again.

**Required.** Keep the filtering logic, remove the silence. When the active
scope excludes bills, show a visible, accurate line such as
"2 bills hidden by the Unpaid view (credit or negative net)" with a control to
show them. Do not change the arithmetic of `billPaymentPosition`.

A filter that removes records without saying so is indistinguishable from data
loss. That is the defect being fixed here.

## Item 3 — Filter by the linked vendor, not the typed name

**Problem.** Two screens answer "whose bill is this?" differently:

- The list matches the **name string**:
  `String(bill.vendorName).toUpperCase() !== filters.vendor.toUpperCase()`
  (`shared/vendorBillFinancialReport.ts`), and server-side
  `eq(vendorBills.vendorName, filters.vendor.toUpperCase())`
  (`server/storage.ts` `getVendorBills`).
- The Previous Billing Summary matches the **linked vendor record**:
  `Number(b.vendorId) === Number(current.vendorId)`
  (`shared/vendorBillReview.ts` `vendorStatementBills`).

So once a vendor master is linked, a bill saved under another spelling of the
same party shows in the finance summary and stays missing from the list. This
master data has at least eight spellings in use.

**Required.** When the selected vendor resolves to a linked vendor record,
filter by that record (plus its aliases, via the existing alias resolution) so
every spelling of that party is returned together. Where a bill has no linked
vendor, keep the existing exact-name behaviour and do not assume that an
unlinked name represents the same party — that rule stays.

Do not merge, rename or edit any vendor master record in this item. This is a
read change only.

## Item 4 — Make the overlap refusal name the blocking bill

**Problem.** Saving a hire bill returns
`409 "This equipment already has an overlapping hire statement"` with nothing
to act on. The user cannot see hire statements anywhere in the application, so
the message is a dead end. The sibling check immediately below it already does
this correctly:

```
Overlapping equipment charge on ${billed.billNo} (${billed.date}); review the
existing bill and choose an unbilled period.
```

**Where.** `server/storage.ts` — the three sites raising
`"This equipment already has an overlapping hire statement"`; surfaced at
`server/routes.ts` (the 409 responder).

**Required.** Include the blocking bill number, its status and the overlapping
dates, in the same shape as the sibling message. Where the overlapping
statement has no parent bill, say that explicitly ("an orphaned hire record
for 26-Aug-2026 to 31-Aug-2026") rather than naming nothing — an orphan is
currently unfindable and must at least be reportable.

Do not relax the check. Do not add any route or action that deletes or detaches
a hire statement.

## Item 6 — Carry the recorded DPR status through to the bill, and split the columns

**Read this item in full before changing anything. An earlier reading of ours
was wrong and we do not want it repeated: the equipment usage status is
already recorded. Do not add a new field for it, and do not ask the site to
enter a reason.**

**What exists.** `equipment_logs.usage_status` and
`equipment_usage.usage_status` already store four values, chosen from a
dropdown in `client/src/components/DprEquipmentCompact.tsx`:

- `working`
- `idle_no_work` — Idle · No Work Available
- `idle_no_operator` — Idle · Operator Unavailable
- `breakdown`

A companion `usage_status_reason` column exists, and
`equipmentStatusRequiresReason` already governs when it is mandatory. Leave
that rule exactly as it is.

**What is broken.** The bill never reads any of it.
`shared/hireBilling.ts`, in `buildHireActivityDays`:

```ts
activity: breakdowns.length ? "breakdown" : dayActivities.length ? "worked" : "no_activity",
```

The day's state is inferred from whether a maintenance record and activity
rows happen to exist. `usageStatus` is not in the `HireActivity` interface and
never enters the pipeline. `HireActivityDay.activity` is a three-value union,
so both kinds of idle collapse into `no_activity`, which
`activityClassification` then reports as "Activity Not Recorded".

Consequence on real bills: `HLC/VB/2026/0056` shows 6 days billed, 6 worked,
**Idle 0, Breakdown 0**. On `HLC/VB/2026/0058` the operator-unavailable days
had to be entered by hand as a manual adjustment
("OPERATOR UNAVAILABLE FOR TWO DAYS (13TH&14TH): −10,000.00"). That manual
line is standing in for this missing connection.

This matters commercially, because the two are opposite: *no work available*
is HLC's own cost and normally payable; *operator unavailable* is the vendor's
and normally deductible.

**Note also** that `activityClassification` in `shared/vendorBillLayout.ts`
already accepts richer vocabulary — `idle`, `work_not_available`,
`operator_unavailable` — that nothing produces. This was evidently intended
and left unfinished. Both ends need to agree, and neither currently matches
the DPR's own words.

**Required.**

1. Carry `usageStatus` (and `usageStatusReason` where present) from the DPR
   and plant usage rows into the hire activity pipeline. `EquipmentStatusRecord`
   in `server/storage.ts` already assembles exactly this from both sources —
   reuse that assembly rather than writing a new query.
2. Widen `HireActivityDay.activity` to carry the four recorded states plus
   "not recorded", and set it from the recorded status. Where a day has a
   recorded status, that status decides; the existing maintenance-record and
   activity-row inference remains only as the fallback for days with no
   recorded status, which must still report as "Activity Not Recorded" and
   never as Idle or Breakdown.
3. Align `activityClassification` with the DPR's actual values. Keep the
   existing accepted spellings for compatibility.
4. Split the single "Breakdown / Opr. Unavailable Days" column into two:
   **Breakdown Days** (machine) and **Operator / Driver Unavailable Days**.
   Update `MONTHLY_BILL_HEADERS` and `hireReviewTable`, and the Excel and PDF
   exports that consume them, together — they share one definition.

**Guard rails for Item 6 — these are not optional.**

- Frozen snapshots stay frozen. A saved statement's
  `calculationSnapshot` is never re-derived on read. New behaviour applies to
  new calculations only.
- No approved, verified or paid bill changes value. Prove it: state
  `HLC/VB/2026/0058`'s net before and after, and show it is identical.
- Do not create any automatic deduction from an operator-unavailable day. This
  item makes the day **visible and correctly labelled**. Whether it is
  deducted stays a human decision through the existing exception and
  adjustment flow. Do not alter deduction arithmetic in this batch.
- No backfill of historical days. Days already recorded keep whatever they
  have. Do not infer a status for a past day from anything.
- Additive migration only if one is needed at all; show the Publish preview
  before publishing.
