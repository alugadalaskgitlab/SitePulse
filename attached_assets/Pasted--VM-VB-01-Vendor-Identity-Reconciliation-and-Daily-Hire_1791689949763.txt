# VM-VB-01 — Vendor Identity Reconciliation and Daily-Hire Tipper Billing

**Priority:** Complete Stages 1 and 2 together. This replaces the earlier unsent VB-TIPPER-02 instruction.

## Objective

Make Vendor Master the authoritative identity for parties across Equipment Master, Material Trip Register and Vendor Bills.

A single party may supply materials, own hired equipment and provide transport. All activities must link to ONE Vendor Master ID while retaining their separate operational and commercial roles.

HLC maintains one Tally creditor ledger per party. Expense classifications will be addressed in a later Tally export phase.

## 1. Vendor Master reconciliation

Extend the existing **Masters → Vendor Master → Link Vendors** functionality.

Include:
- Equipment Master hired-equipment owners
- Material Trip transport suppliers
- Material Trip material suppliers
- Vendor Bills and existing party references where applicable

Show the recorded name, suggested Vendor Master, vehicle registration where relevant, source record count and any conflict.

Allow Administrator/Owner confirmation of the correct vendor.

Use existing stable vendor IDs. Add a nullable Vendor Master reference to Equipment Master only if genuinely necessary, through a safe additive migration.

**Do not merge vendors, overwrite historical names, or automatically confirm uncertain matches.**

Multiple equipment records must be linkable to the same Vendor Master.

Examples to verify, without assuming identity:
- NARASIMHULU → GANGARAM NARSIMHULU
- SANGANNA → G SANGANNA
- FASIUDDIN → MD FASIUDDIN
- MAHIPAL → MAHIPAL KALPAGURI

Preserve any existing bill-specific vendor references.

## 2. Material supplier versus transporter

Maintain independent identities for:
- Material seller/source
- Transport vendor
- Hired equipment owner

Own Source means HLC's material source; it does not mean company-owned transportation.

Do not alter historical material receipts, supplier names, trip quantities, vehicle numbers or receipt references.

For future transactions, prefer selecting an existing Vendor Master entry. Preserve manual-name entry where required, but clearly identify unlinked names.

## 3. Fix confirmed tipper discovery

A confirmed Material Trip → Equipment Master hire link must become visible in Equipment Hire Vendor Bills under the correct linked Vendor Master, even if historical names differ.

Fix the vendor-filtered retrieval, frontend options and query refresh together.

If linked evidence is still excluded, show its exact reason: missing Vendor Master link, missing daily-hire terms, invalid confirmation, wrong vendor, outside hire period, already billed, or another genuine restriction.

Do not ask engineers to re-enter the same trips into submitted DPRs.

## 4. Daily-hire billing

Reuse VB-TIPPER-01 and the existing hire calculator.

- One vehicle/date = at most one payable hire day.
- Combine DPR equipment logs, standalone usage and confirmed Material Trips without double-charging.
- Treat trip counts as supporting evidence, never as automatic full-day entitlement.
- Allow Full Day, Half Day and Excluded Day with recorded reasons.
- Preserve agreed daily rates and hire periods.
- Detect prior billed activity and overlapping hire statements.
- Do not fabricate operating hours, meter readings or diesel consumption.

## 5. Keep accounting identity separate

One Vendor Master represents one party, regardless of transaction type.

Do not create separate Vendor Master records merely because the party supplies materials and hires equipment.

Do not implement Tally export yet. Do not add expense-ledger accounting rules in this batch.

## 6. Financial/data safeguards

Do not rewrite:
- Existing Vendor Bills, paid bills or hire snapshots
- DPRs and equipment logs
- Material Trip history
- Stock/diesel transactions
- Existing supplier rates or hire terms
- Purchase Indents, Purchase Orders or receipts

If schema changes are required, use only additive, nondestructive changes. No mass historical backfill.

All vendor linking must be authorised, reviewable and auditable.

## 7. Verification

Use representative real-world data structures with differing supplier names.

Test:
1. Several tippers from the same vendor, with different historical owner-name spellings.
2. One Vendor Master for both material supply and equipment hire.
3. HLC Own Source material carried by a hired tipper.
4. Previously confirmed tipper becoming available in the correct Vendor Bill.
5. Several trips and a DPR equipment log on the same date resulting in one hire day.
6. Full, half and excluded days.
7. Incorrect vendor association and ambiguous identities.
8. Already-billed or paid days.
9. Existing unrelated Vendor Bills and historical records unaffected.

Run focused tests and one production build. No full-suite testing or unrelated refactoring.

Implement the complete working flow. Provide one concise completion report stating changes, test results, migrations and steps for verifying one real vendor/tipper.

Do not publish automatically or change production records during development. STOP.
