WHY
HLC buys material from one party and sometimes hires a different party to haul it. site_material_trips already separates the two roles (shared/schema.ts ~902):
  - materialSourceSupplier / materialSourceVendorId = who SOLD the material
  - supplier / supplierVendorId = the TRANSPORTER (the vehicle side) — the schema comment says this explicitly
  - transportType = "in_house" | "agency_vendor"
  - internalEquipmentId = the own-fleet machine when in_house
But the entry form does not ask the question clearly, so the two roles are recorded inconsistently and older trips are ambiguous. This batch fixes the entry and the display only.

1. When the SAME party supplies and delivers, BOTH vendor columns hold that same party. Never leave the transporter blank to mean "same" — blank is indistinguishable from "not recorded".
2. NO automatic backfill or correction of historical trips. The owner is reviewing them by hand. Not one existing row may be rewritten.
3. In-house transport (own tipper) has no transport vendor.
