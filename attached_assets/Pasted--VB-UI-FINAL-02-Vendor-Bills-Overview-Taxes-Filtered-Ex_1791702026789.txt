# VB-UI-FINAL-02 — Vendor Bills Overview, Taxes & Filtered Excel Export

**Objective:** Simplify the existing Vendor Bills page based on live user feedback. Implement the following as ONE focused task, using existing components, filters, permissions and financial calculations.

Do not redesign Vendor Bills or introduce additional financial panels.

## 1. Unpaid / All Bills selector

Add one small selector:

**Unpaid | All Bills**

Default: Unpaid.

The selector must control the Vendor Bill list, financial summary, taxes summary and exports together.

**Unpaid:**
- Include Draft, Verified and Approved bills with a genuine outstanding positive balance.
- Include partially paid bills where an amount remains outstanding, regardless of their workflow status.
- Exclude fully settled bills.
- Do not classify bills by status alone.
- Where a bill is marked Paid but reliable payment evidence contradicts that status, flag the inconsistency briefly and include it if a positive balance remains.
- Where historical payment evidence is missing, retain the bill as **Unconfirmed** rather than assuming it is settled or treating an unknown payment as zero.
- Show negative/credit bills through All Bills, without automatically offsetting them against other bills.

**All Bills:**
- Include all saved bills, irrespective of status or payment position.

Apply all existing filters—vendor, site, date, status, category and others—to the chosen Unpaid/All scope.

Do not add duplicate filter controls.

## 2. Compact Financial Summary

Immediately above the existing filters/list, show one compact horizontal row:

| No. of Bills | Gross Bill Amount | Paid Amount | Net Payable / Outstanding |
|---|---|---|---|

All four totals must represent exactly the bills currently visible under Unpaid/All and the existing filters.

Use existing authoritative financial totals and recorded payment amounts.

For the fourth figure, sum only established positive outstanding balances. Do not silently offset negative/credit balances across bills or vendors.

Draft and Verified balances may be included but are provisional, not authorised for payment. Explain this using a brief caption or tooltip.

If records contain missing or contradictory payment information, use a small **Unconfirmed** indicator with a count. Do not add elaborate warnings, separate reconciliation panels or assumptions.

Validate arithmetic against actual bill detail calculations, including advance deductions, additional adjustments, GST, TDS and partial payments.

Do not infer that Paid status alone proves the amount actually paid.

## 3. Compact Taxes Summary — retain category columns

Keep the existing category structure and display a small taxes summary, not a bill-wise register.

Use the layout:

| Tax | Equipment | Material | Transport | Labour | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GST | Amount | Amount | Amount | Amount | Total |
| TDS (IT) | Amount | Amount | Amount | Amount | Total |
| Total | Amount | Amount | Amount | Amount | Grand Total |

- Use actual recorded GST and TDS amounts only.
- Do not calculate TDS from a saved TDS percentage when the deducted amount was not recorded.
- Preserve the existing GST calculations and category allocation.
- Distinguish Not established from genuine zero.
- The Total row adds the displayed tax amounts for reporting only; it is not another tax or an additional bill liability.
- No bill-wise listing or long TDS register table on the main Vendor Bills page.
- No additional tax filters.

All tax figures must respond to the same Unpaid/All selection and existing Vendor Bill filters.

Detailed GST/TDS rates and bill-wise information belong in the Excel export.

## 4. One filtered Excel export

Provide one **Export Financial Summary** action near the compact summary.

The Excel workbook must contain three sheets:

**Sheet 1 — Vendor Bills**
- Bill ID and number
- Bill date and vendor
- Site, type/category and status
- Gross amount
- GST
- Adjustments/deductions
- Net bill value
- Recorded paid amount
- Positive outstanding or negative credit
- Small indicator for unconfirmed payment evidence

**Sheet 2 — GST Register**
- Existing bill-wise GST information
- Equipment, Material, Transport and Labour classifications
- Applicable recorded GST rates and amounts
- Relevant totals

**Sheet 3 — TDS (IT) Register**
- Bill-wise TDS information
- Applicable recorded TDS rate
- Recorded taxable/base amount, if available
- Actual recorded deduction amount
- Relevant totals
- Not established where the deducted amount was not recorded

**Critical:** All three sheets must contain the same selected bills as the visible Vendor Bill list.

Unpaid/All and all existing filters must apply to the export.

Use existing trusted export utilities. Do not add a second independent vendor/date/status selection for exporting.

Include totals and a brief note when payment or TDS information is incomplete.

Do not change existing Vendor Bill PDF, Activity Calendar PDF/Excel, or other operational exports.
