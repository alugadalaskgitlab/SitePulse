The bug: "Re-map All" calls remapBoqProject, which blanket-resets every item to "unmapped" and re-runs the auto-mapper — and the auto-mapper overwrites/ignores manual mappings. That's why your manual work vanished.

The good news: the no-match branch only flips the status flag — it does not delete your manual mapping rows. So your manual mappings (the cross-category ones the auto-mapper can't re-find, like HYSD/tree-cutting) are still in the database, just hidden. I confirmed manual maps are tagged isAutoMapped=false, so I can protect and recover them.
