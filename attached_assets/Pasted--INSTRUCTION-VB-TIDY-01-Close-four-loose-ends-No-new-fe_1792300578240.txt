## Part A — Give the complete export a way in

**The fault.** `GET /api/vendor-bills/export?format=json|xlsx` exists and works, but
**nothing in the app reaches it.** The only way to run it is to type the address by
hand. Finished work that cannot be reached is the pattern this batch is closing.

**Required.**

1. A control on the Vendor Bills page — beside the existing export action — that
   downloads the complete export. Offer both the workbook and the JSON.
2. It is shown **only** to a user who would pass the endpoint's own checks:
   Administrator or Owner **and** holding View Vendor Financials. A user who would be
   refused does not see the control.
3. It passes the filters currently in force on the page, and labels plainly that it is
   the complete bill export, distinct from the existing Taxes Register export.

**Constraints.** Do not change the endpoint, its permission checks, its site scoping,
or the assembly in `shared/vendorBillExport.ts`. Do not change the existing
`report=financial-summary` export in any way.

## Part E — Make the small text readable

**The fault.** Secondary and helper text across the app is set small enough that the
owner has to zoom the browser to read it. Examples he has hit: the per-vendor note on
the payables table ("Estimate · 12 payment unknown"), the metric captions under the
headline figures, and the meta line under each bill card. This is not a complaint
about style — these lines carry meaning about money, and a figure whose caveat cannot
be read is worse than no caveat.

**Required.**

1. Raise the smallest text sizes used for **meaningful secondary text** to a readable
   minimum. In practice that means the `text-[10px]` and `text-xs` usages that carry
   information — captions, notes, meta lines, table sub-labels, badge text — move up
   one step.
2. Check the contrast of those same lines. Grey-on-white at a small size is the
   combination that fails; where a line is both small and faint, fix both.
3. Apply it **consistently**, through the shared styles or components rather than page
   by page, so new pages inherit it.

**Constraints.**

- **Do not redesign anything.** No new colours, no new fonts, no spacing overhaul, no
  layout changes. This is size and contrast on existing text.
- Headline figures, table numbers and headings stay exactly as they are.
- Nothing may reflow so that a table scrolls horizontally where it did not before, or
  a figure wraps mid-number. Check the payables table and the bill cards specifically
  at the laptop width the owner uses.
- No text is removed or shortened to make room.
