name: Tally cutover ownership
description: User-specified cutoff for Tally ownership and vendor-bill period placement.
---

Tally owns vendor-bill periods ending on or before 2026-01-31. Periods starting after 2026-01-31 are post-cutover; valid periods that extend beyond the cutoff but start on or before it are straddling. Invalid, missing, and reversed periods remain period-not-recorded.

**Why:** The user identified 2026-01-31 as the last Tally-owned day, with SitePulse billing beginning 2026-02-01.

**How to apply:** Use this inclusive cutoff when classifying vendor-bill readiness periods; do not infer placement from bill date.
